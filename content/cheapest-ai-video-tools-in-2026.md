## Kling AI: Usually the Cheapest Per Clip

Kling (by Kuaishou) has consistently undercut Western competitors on raw cost-per-second of generated video, largely because it monetizes through China-market scale and a simpler credit system.

| Plan | Price | Credits/mo | Approx. Clips (5s, standard) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Daily refresh (~66 credits/day) | 10–15/mo |
| Standard | ~$10/mo | 660 credits | ~60 clips |
| Pro | ~$30/mo | 3,000 credits | ~270 clips |
| Premier | ~$92/mo | 8,000+ credits | ~700+ clips |

**Real-dollar takeaway:** At the Standard tier, you're paying roughly **$0.10–$0.17 per 5-second clip**, which is the lowest in this comparison. The catch: higher-quality modes (1080p, longer duration, "Pro" model) burn credits 2–4x faster, so your effective cost climbs quickly if you need polish.

## Common Pricing Traps

1. **"Credits" ≠ fixed cost.** A 5-second clip at 720p might cost 10 credits; the same clip at 1080p or with a "Pro" model can cost 30–40 credits. Always check the per-generation cost *before* you commit to a plan size.

2. **Annual vs. monthly billing gaps.** Runway and Luma both show ~20–25% lower effective pricing on annual plans — but you're locked in even if the model roadmap changes or a competitor drops pricing.

3. **"Unlimited" isn't unlimited.** Runway and Luma's top tiers use soft fair-use caps. Heavy users report throttling or queue deprioritization after generating hundreds of clips in a short window.

4. **Resolution upcharges.** Several tools quietly reserve 4K/HD output for Pro+ tiers or charge 2–3x credits for it — factor this in if your use case needs more than 720p drafts.

5. **Free tier watermarks aren't always removable at the cheapest paid tier.** Pika, in particular, keeps watermarks on its Standard plan — you need Pro to go watermark-free.

6. **Rollover doesn't always exist.** Unused monthly credits on most of these platforms **do not roll over
