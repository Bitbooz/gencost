文章內容

AI avatar video tools let you make talking-head videos without a camera, studio or presenter. In 2026 the real question is which one costs the least for the videos you actually make.

**HeyGen 定價（GenCost 資料）**
- 免費方案：$0/月，3 部影片
- Creator：$29/月，600 點數
- Pro：$49/月，1000 點數

**Synthesia 定價（GenCost 資料）**
- Basic：$0/月，500 點數
- Starter：$19/月，1250 點數
- Pro：$89/月，6000 點數

**數字顯示什麼**
- **最便宜的付費入門方案：** Synthesia Starter 每月 $19，比 HeyGen Creator 每月 $29 便宜 $10，而且包含更多點數（1250 比 600）。
- **免費方案：** HeyGen 的免費方案以影片數量限制（3 部影片）。Synthesia 的免費 Basic 方案以點數限制（500）。付費前請兩邊都測試。
- **中階方案跳躍：** HeyGen Pro 每月 $49 可得 1000 點數。Synthesia Pro 每月 $89 可得 6000 點數，適合高產量團隊。
- **每點數粗估成本：** HeyGen Creator 約 $0.048 每點數，Pro 約 $0.049。Synthesia Starter 約 $0.015，Pro 約 $0.015。

**點數使用注意事項：** 每個平台對點數的定義與消耗方式不同，一個工具的點數不等於另一個工具的點數。請將每點數成本視為參考值，並在各工具上實際跑一段 1 分鐘的測試影片，確認真實成本。

**Colossyan 與 Elai：** GenCost 目前未收錄這兩家的定價，因此這裡不提供數字。請自行查看最新定價頁面，並用相同標準比較：每完成分鐘成本、浮水印規則、虛擬人品質、支援語言與輸出限制。

**快速推薦**
- **最低每月花費：** Synthesia Starter（$19/月）
- **先試用再決定：** HeyGen 免費方案（$0，3 部影片）或 Synthesia Basic（$0，500 點數）
- **大量製作：** Synthesia Pro（$89/月，6000 點數）
- **HeyGen 中階選擇：** Pro（$49/月，1000 點數）

購買前請務必到各官方網站確認最新方案，因為價格經常變動。

描述

Which AI avatar video tool is actually cheapest in 2026? We compare HeyGen, Synthesia, Colossyan and Elai, using real GenCost pricing for HeyGen and Synthesia.

腳本大綱

- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** "AI avatar tools range from $0 to $89 a month. Which one is actually the cheapest for you?" Show the four logos and a price ticker on screen.
- **Criteria (0:30–1:15):** Explain what counts: monthly price, credits or video limits, watermarks, avatar realism, language support, export quality. Promise a simple recommendation at the end.
- **HeyGen (1:15–2:30):** Free is $0 for 3 videos. Creator is $29/mo for 600 credits. Pro is $49/mo for 1000 credits. Show the dashboard, create a 1-minute test video and note how many credits it used.
- **Synthesia (2:30–3:45):** Basic is $0 for 500 credits. Starter is $19/mo for 1250 credits. Pro is $89/mo for 6000 credits. Run the same test script and record the credit usage.
- **Colossyan and Elai (3:45–4:45):** Be upfront that these prices aren't in our dataset. Show their pricing pages live, flag that prices change, and apply the same criteria to both.
- **Cost comparison (4:45–6:00):** Show a table of plan, price and credits. Point out that Synthesia Starter is $10 cheaper than HeyGen Creator with more credits. Explain the rough per-credit math, then warn that credits aren't equal across tools. Compare the cost of the same test video on each.
- **Who should pick what (6:00–6:45):**
  - Beginner testing: free tiers
  - Solo creator on a budget: Synthesia Starter
  - Prefer HeyGen's workflow: Creator or Pro
  - Teams with volume: Synthesia Pro
- **Tips and CTA (6:45–7:15):** Batch your videos, keep scripts tight, cancel when you're not producing. Ask viewers which tool they use, and point to the pricing links in the description.
