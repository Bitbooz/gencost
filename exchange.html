      How it works: a tool's price is the average, over its paid plans, of the cheapest way to pay each month (billed monthly, or yearly per month when that is cheaper). A new price only counts after GenCost has read it on the official pricing page on two different days. GCX is the average of every tool's index (each tool starts at 100), times ten.
