Frequently Asked Questions
Is Dreamina free to use?

Yes. Dreamina offers a free option that lets you explore its AI creation tools with available credits. You can also upgrade to a paid plan for more credits and additional plan benefits.

What is the difference between Dreamina Free, Basic, Standard, and Advanced plans?

Dreamina plans mainly differ in the number of credits and benefits included. Free is suitable for trying out Dreamina, while Basic, Standard, and Advanced plans are designed for creators with increasing generation needs.

How do Dreamina credits work?

Dreamina credits are used to generate AI images, videos, and other creative content. The number of credits required may vary depending on the model, feature, and generation settings you use.

Which Dreamina plan should I choose?

Choose a plan based on how often you create and how many credits you expect to use. Occasional creators can start with a lower-tier plan, while frequent users may benefit from plans with more credits.

Can I upgrade or change my Dreamina plan?

Yes, you can choose a different Dreamina plan as your creative needs change. Check the pricing options on this page for the latest plans, benefits, and billing details.

Can I cancel my Dreamina subscription?

You can manage your Dreamina subscription through your account and billing settings. Cancellation and renewal terms may vary depending on your subscription method.

Can I use Dreamina creations for commercial purposes?

Commercial-use eligibility depends on the applicable Dreamina terms, the content you create, and the models or assets involved. Before using Dreamina-generated content commercially, review the latest Terms of Service and relevant usage requirements.
