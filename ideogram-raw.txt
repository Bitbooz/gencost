Basic plan:
Ideogram no longer sells the Basic plan ($8/month or $7/month billed yearly). If you already have it, you keep it and get 960 credits each month.
What changes besides the credit allowance?
Plus includes private generation and quality export. Pro adds
Batch Generation
and a larger generation queue. Team adds central billing and administration, with credits assigned to each member. Enterprise features and allowances follow the organization's agreement.
Open the plan comparison under
Settings
→
Plans
to review the features before choosing. Buying more credits funds usage; it doesn't change your subscription tier.
Does pay as you go include monthly credits?
Pay as you go uses credits you buy as needed, without a monthly subscription allowance. Credits pay for eligible image, video and API usage.
Auto recharge
is a separate option for replenishing the wallet.
How credits work
Images, videos, edits, upscales and app runs all cost credits. The cost depends on the model, quality, size and number of outputs. In most image apps, the run button shows the cost before you start. In the Video app, the
Generate
button shows credits once your inputs are ready and a quote is available. A
~
marks an estimate; hover over the number to see whether the quote is estimated or exact.
To see what everything costs, including video, go to
Settings
→
Usage costs
.

Enterprise Prepaid

After your credits run out
What happens depends on your plan:
Plan
When your credits run out
Free
Wait for next week's 30 credits, or
buy credits
.
Pay as you go
Buy more credits
, or turn on auto recharge so your balance refills itself.
Basic
Keep using Ideogram models in a slower queue, up to a daily limit.
Plus, Pro, Team
Keep using Ideogram models in a slower queue, with no limit.
On Plus, Pro and Team you can keep generating with Ideogram models after your credits run out. These generations wait in a slower queue. These still need credits:
custom models;
partner models;
generations that use a reference image;
image edits;
upscales.
On Basic, Plus, Pro and Team, buy extra credits or wait for your next renewal to keep using them.
Free plan
Free accounts get 30 credits each week if they sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft. Accounts created with an email and password don't get free credits; they can
buy credits
or subscribe.
On the free plan, everything you generate is public. Plus, Pro, Team and Enterprise keep new images private until you publish them; legacy Basic doesn't.
