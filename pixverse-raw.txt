Frequently Asked Questions
Why Subscribe?
Our subscription plans unlock premium features to elevate your creative experience. Benefits vary by plan and may include:
- Higher-resolution outputs, up to 4K
- Watermark-free & Ad-free
- Access to more powerful video and image models
- Faster generation speeds and more concurrent generations
- Bonus Credits with Credit Pack purchases
- Unlimited free image generations in Relax Mode on select models (available with eligible plans)
- Savings on Credits in select generation modes
Choose the plan that fits your creative needs and unlock more with PixVerse.
What are the Credits?
Credits are used for creating videos at PixVerse.
1) Daily Credits renew every day at 0AM UTC and expire at the end of the day if unused.
2) Membership Credits refresh every 30 days from the exact date and time your subscription began, rather than at 0:00 UTC. Any unused Membership Credits expire when the new 30-day cycle begins.
3) Bonus Credits are extra Credits obtained through purchases, rewards, and promotional events. These Credits never expire and will stay in your account until used.
How do I get more Credits?
There are multiple ways to obtain more Credits:
1) Collect Daily Credits by logging in.
2) Get Membership Credits by subscribing.
3) Purchase Bonus Credits or earn Bonus Credits through promotional events and activities.
How can I delete my account?
We're sorry to hear you'd like to delete your account. If you wish to proceed, you can delete your account through the Delete Account option in User Info. Please back up any data you want to keep first. Your account will be permanently deleted after 5 days, and your subscription will be canceled at that time. After that, you will not be charged, and no future renewals will occur. During the 5-day cooling-off period, please cancel auto-renewal yourself if you don't want further charges. If your subscription was purchased through the App Store, it won't be canceled automatically. Please manage or cancel it in your App Store subscription settings. We'd be happy to welcome you back anytime!
How can I get a refund?
Please understand that AI-generated video results may be unstable and do not guarantee complete fulfillment of expected outcomes. We encourage you to make full use of the free Credits provided for your experience. We do not accept refund requests based on dissatisfaction with the generated results. More details about our refund policy:
- Partial refunds are not supported.
- Only subscriptions that are still within their validity period and have not been used, including annual and monthly plans, are eligible for a full refund. Expired subscriptions are not refundable, even if unused.
- Credit Pack purchases are non-refundable, regardless of whether the Credits have been used.
For refund inquiries, please contact us at support@pixverse.ai with your registered email, account ID, payment receipt, and purchased product details.
