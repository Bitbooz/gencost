Cheapest clip finder

Where is one AI video clip cheapest?

Pick a model, quality and clip length. GenCost works out what one clip costs on every platform that offers it, from today's plan prices and each platform's own credits per clip, and puts the cheapest first.

Cost per clip = plan price ÷ plan credits × credits for one clip, using either the best-value paid plan or the smallest paid plan on each platform that includes the model. "Rough" means the platform only gives an estimate, or the length was worked out from another clip length. Prices from GenCost data, updated . Always confirm inside the tool before you pay.

Put GenCost on your phone. On Android, open the menu (☰) and tap Install app, or use Chrome's menu → Add to Home screen. On iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Prices update every morning, the same as the website.
