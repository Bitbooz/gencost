How the calculator works

  • Video: each tool's own credits per clip, for its default model and resolution, times the number of clips and tries. A plan counts only if its monthly credits cover the whole month.
  • Voice: about 1,000 characters make one minute of speech.
  • Music and images: each tool's own track, download or image allowance.
  • Only paid plans are counted: free plans add watermarks or forbid commercial use. Tools whose allowance can't be converted reliably are left out instead of guessed.

Prices are verified every morning from each official pricing page. Results are estimates; credits per clip and per image change with model, length and quality. See the video, voice, music and image price lists for every plan.
