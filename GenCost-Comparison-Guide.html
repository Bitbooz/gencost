HeyGen vs Higgsfield (October 2026)

HeyGen is usually the better and cheaper choice if you mainly want classic talking-head videos or AI avatars.
If you stick to the older Avatar III model, a minute of video can cost as little as about $0.20. The newer Avatar V and cinematic features get expensive quickly (often $2+ per minute).

Higgsfield is stronger when you want modern generative video (Kling, Seedance, and similar models).
The higher plans — especially if you pay yearly — make each credit noticeably cheaper. This is the better option if you care more about cinematic or multi-model video than pure talking avatars.

Important tip for everyone:
Always look at the credit cost shown inside the app before you hit generate. Both platforms change their rates regularly.
Simple recommendation:
  • Mainly need talking avatars / AI influencer videos ? Start with HeyGen Creator
  • Want more cinematic or multi-model video ? Look at Higgsfield Plus or Ultra on yearly billing

Prices are approximate based on October 2026 data and can change.
