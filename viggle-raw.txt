FAQs
How do Viggle credits work?
Credits are used across Viggle’s generation tools and models. Each feature may use a different number of credits, depending on the model and output.
Do credits expire?
Monthly plan credits expire on your billing date. Purchased credits do not expire, but they can only be used when you are subscribed to a Viggle plan.
Can I cancel anytime?
You can cancel your subscription at any time. However, the cancellation will take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. If you change your mind, you can restore the subscription before the cycle ends.
Does Viggle provide API services?
Yes! Our API powers character swaps, avatar experiences, social apps, game workflows, and branded campaigns at scale.
Check out our API page to learn more
Are Viggle AI and PINOC billed separately?
Yes. PINOC is billed separately from Viggle AI, so each product has its own plan.
