GenCost Quick Guide – HeyGen vs Higgsfield

HeyGen is usually better and cheaper for classic talking-head / avatar videos (especially Avatar III ˜ $0.20/min). Avatar V and cinematic features get expensive fast.

Higgsfield is stronger for generative video (Kling, Seedance, etc.). Higher plans (especially yearly) make credits cheaper.

Tip: Always check the credit cost inside the app before generating. Rates change.

Recommendation:
• Talking avatars ? HeyGen Creator
• Cinematic / multi-model ? Higgsfield Plus or Ultra (yearly)
