Pricing questions, answered
Can’t find your answer?
Contact us
and we’ll help.
What is a credit?
Credits are your one balance for every model on Elyum. Each video or image costs a set number of credits, so you can mix models freely, and the exact price shows on the button before you generate. A credit is 1¢ on Plus and cheaper on bigger plans: down to 0.69¢ on Ultra, and 0.61¢ billed yearly.
How many videos or images do I get?
It depends on the model, the quality and the length. Pick a plan, a quality and a length in What your plan creates above and it counts every model from our live rate card. Every take arrives as a preview first, and a take you kill gives its credits back, so your count only goes down for the takes you keep.
What happens if I don't like a result?
Kill it. Its credits come back to your balance at once and nothing is charged. Every plan includes kills each month: 2 on Plus, 10 on Pro and 25 on Ultra. A failed render refunds itself and never uses a kill.
How many kills do I get?
2 on Plus, 10 on Pro and 25 on Ultra each billing period, however many credits you buy. Most takes use 1 kill. Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 (not Fast or Mini) use 1 at 480p, 2 at 720p and 4 at 1080p (Seedance 2.0 at 4K uses 8); Wan 3.0 and Kling 4.0 use 2 at 1080p and 1 below. The Kill button always shows how many. Keeps and failed renders never use one, and a preview never expires: a take you have not decided on waits, with its credits on hold.
Which models are free?
On Pro and Ultra the everyday image models render at zero credits (NB 2 Lite and Z-Image Turbo), and Ultra adds the mid models (Nano Banana 2 up to 1K). Free renders are fair use and only made in the studio, in your browser: not through the API, MCP or the Elyum Agent, which can render the same models on credits. They run one at a time in a shared queue, so they can take much longer than a render on credits. Video always uses credits.
Do higher resolutions cost more?
Yes. A higher resolution costs more to run, so video is priced by the second at the quality you pick and images by their size. Draft at 480p, then keep the take you want. What your plan creates shows each model's price at every quality and length.
Do unused credits roll over?
Plan credits are good for the billing month they land in; at renewal what is left expires and the plan's full grant arrives fresh. Credits you buy as a top-up never expire, and your plan's credits are always spent first.
How do yearly plans work?
You pay once for the year and save up to two months against paying monthly. Your credits and kills still arrive every month and expire like a monthly plan's, and the plan's price and monthly credits are fixed for your year. We email you a month before it renews, and you can turn renewal off any time from Billing. Within 14 days of buying, if you haven't used its credits, we refund the year in full; after that it isn't refunded in part. Discount codes apply to monthly billing.
Can I switch or cancel?
Any time. Upgrades apply at once, and cancelling stops the renewal at the end of the period, with your balance live until then. On a yearly plan you can move up to a bigger yearly plan whenever you like, and the months your year hasn't delivered come off the new price. Everything you kept stays yours, commercial use included.
What do I get on Free?
Free generation is paused for now because of supplier costs. Your account, saved assets and kept downloads stay available, and any paid plan can keep creating.
