How we verify prices

GenCost converts credit systems into approximate real-dollar prices so you can compare AI video, voice and music tools side-by-side.

What we do

  • We check official pricing pages regularly.
  • We translate credits into approximate dollar costs (usually per 10 seconds).
  • We update the site automatically when prices change.
  • We show the last update time on the homepage.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t rank quality or claim one tool is “best”.
  • We don’t hide that prices change often.
  • We don’t use affiliate links that affect the numbers we show.

Always double-check

Prices change frequently. Always confirm the current price inside the tool before you buy or generate.
