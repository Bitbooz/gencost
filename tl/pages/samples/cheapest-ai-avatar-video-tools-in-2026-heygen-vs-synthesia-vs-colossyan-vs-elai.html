1. Article

Title: Pinakamurang AI Avatar Video Tools sa 2026

Meta description: Ihambing ang HeyGen, Synthesia, Colossyan at Elai sa 2026. Tingnan ang tunay na GenCost pricing para sa HeyGen at Synthesia, pati na ang dapat suriin bago magbayad.

Article content

Pinapayagan ka ng AI avatar video tools na gumawa ng talking-head videos nang walang camera, studio o presenter. Sa 2026 ang tunay na tanong ay alin ang pinakamura para sa mga videos na talagang ginagawa mo.

**HeyGen pricing (GenCost data)**
- Free: $0/buwan, 3 videos
- Creator: $29/buwan, 600 credits
- Pro: $49/buwan, 1000 credits

**Synthesia pricing (GenCost data)**
- Basic: $0/buwan, 500 credits
- Starter: $19/buwan, 1250 credits
- Pro: $89/buwan, 6000 credits

**What the numbers show**
- **Cheapest paid entry:** Synthesia Starter sa $19/buwan ay $10 mas mura kaysa HeyGen Creator sa $29/buwan, at mas marami pang credits ang kasama (1250 vs 600).
- **Free tiers:** HeyGen free plan ay limitado sa bilang ng video (3 videos). Synthesia free Basic plan ay limitado sa credits (500). Subukan mo muna ang dalawa bago magbayad.
- **Mid-tier jump:** HeyGen Pro ay nagkakahalaga ng $49/buwan para sa 1000 credits. Synthesia Pro ay $89/buwan para sa 6000 credits, na bagay sa high-volume teams.
- **Rough cost per credit:** HeyGen Creator ay halos $0.048 kada credit at Pro ay halos $0.049. Synthesia Starter ay halos $0.015 at Pro ay halos $0.015.

**A caveat on credits:** Bawat platform ay iba ang pag-define at pag-consume ng credits, kaya hindi pantay ang credit ng isang tool sa isa pa. Ituring mo lang na rough guide ang per-credit figures at subukan mo ang parehong 1-minute test video sa bawat tool para makita ang tunay na cost.

**Colossyan and Elai:** Hindi kasama sa package na ito ang GenCost pricing ng dalawang ito, kaya wala kaming binabanggit na numbers para sa kanila. Tingnan mo ang kanilang current pricing pages at ikumpara sa parehong criteria: cost per finished minute, watermark rules, avatar quality, languages at export limits.

**Quick picks**
- **Lowest monthly spend:** Synthesia Starter ($19/buwan)
- **Trying before buying:** HeyGen Free ($0, 3 videos) o Synthesia Basic ($0, 500 credits)
- **Heavy production:** Synthesia Pro ($89/buwan, 6000 credits)
- **Middle ground on HeyGen:** Pro ($49/buwan, 1000 credits)

Laging kumpirmahin ang current plans sa site ng bawat vendor bago bumili, dahil madalas nagbabago ang pricing.

Description

Which AI avatar video tool is actually cheapest in 2026? We compare HeyGen, Synthesia, Colossyan and Elai, using real GenCost pricing for HeyGen and Synthesia.

Script outline

- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** "AI avatar tools range from $0 to $89 a month. Which one is actually the cheapest for you?" Show the four logos and a price ticker on screen.
- **Criteria (0:30–1:15):** Explain what counts: monthly price, credits or video limits, watermarks, avatar realism, language support, export quality. Promise a simple recommendation at the end.
- **HeyGen (1:15–2:30):** Free is $0 for 3 videos. Creator is $29/mo for 600 credits. Pro is $49/mo for 1000 credits. Show the dashboard, create a 1-minute test video and note how many credits it used.
- **Synthesia (2:30–3:45):** Basic is $0 for 500 credits. Starter is $19/mo for 1250 credits. Pro is $89/mo for 6000 credits. Run the same test script and record the credit usage.
- **Colossyan and Elai (3:45–4:45):** Be upfront that these prices aren't in our dataset. Show their pricing pages live, flag that prices change, and apply the same criteria to both.
- **Cost comparison (4:45–6:00):** Show a table of plan, price and credits. Point out that Synthesia Starter is $10 cheaper than HeyGen Creator with more credits. Explain the rough per-credit math, then warn that credits aren't equal across tools. Compare the cost of the same test video on each.
- **Who should pick what (6:00–6:45):**
  - Beginner testing: free tiers
  - Solo creator on a budget: Synthesia Starter
  - Prefer HeyGen's workflow: Creator or Pro
  - Teams with volume: Synthesia Pro
- **Tips and CTA (6:45–7:15):** Batch your videos, keep scripts tight, cancel when you're not producing. Ask viewers which tool they use, and point to the pricing links in the description.
