1. Article

Pamagat: Pinakamurang AI Video Upscaling Tools sa 2026: Topaz vs Magnific vs Runway vs CapCut

Meta description: Ikumpara ang AI video upscaling at enhancement tools sa 2026. Tingnan ang real Runway pricing, kung paano nagkakaiba ang Topaz, Magnific at CapCut, at alin ang bagay sa budget at workflow mo.

Article content

Puwedeng gawing mas matalas at malinis ng AI video upscaling ang soft 720p o 1080p footage. Pero iba-iba ang charging ng apat na tools dito, kaya ang pinakamura ay depende sa kung gaano karaming footage ang ipoproseso mo.

**Paano nagkakaiba ang pricing models**
- **Runway** ay gumagamit ng monthly plans na may credits. Ginagastos ang credits kada generation o processing task, kaya sumasabay ang cost sa usage.
- **Topaz** ay desktop software na nakatuon sa local processing. Bagay ito sa mga taong madalas mag-upscale at gusto ng control sa resulta. Tingnan mo ang site ng Topaz para sa current licensing at pricing.
- **Magnific** ay kilala sa creative, detail-adding AI enhancement. Tingnan mo ang site ng Magnific para sa current plans at limits.
- **CapCut** ay all-in-one editor na may AI enhancement features, at may free at paid tiers. Tingnan mo ang app para sa kung ano ang kasama sa bawat isa.

**Runway pricing (from GenCost data)**
| Plan | Price | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0/buwan | 125 credits |
| Standard | $12/buwan | 625 credits |
| Pro | $28/buwan | 2,250 credits |
| Max | $76/buwan | 9,500 credits |

Per credit, ang mga plan ay nasa roughly $0.019 (Standard), $0.012 (Pro) at $0.008 (Max). Mas mura ang cost per credit sa heavier plans, pero kapag talagang ginagamit mo ang credits.

**Alin ang pinakamura para sa'yo?**
- **Occasional clips:** Simulan mo sa free tiers at Runway Free plan (125 credits) para i-test ang quality bago magbayad.
- **Regular creators:** Runway Standard sa $12/buwan ay low-commitment option. Ang Pro sa $28/buwan ay bagay kapag nauubos mo ang credits.
- **Batch or archival work:** Desktop software tulad ng Topaz ay mas magandang value kapag marami kang footage na ipoproseso, dahil hindi ka metered per clip.
- **Quick social edits:** Maginhawa ang CapCut kung nag-eedit ka na doon.

**Bottom line:** I-test mo ang parehong 10-second clip sa bawat tool at ikumpara ang resulta sa kung magkano ang babayaran mo kada buwan. Madalas nagbabago ang prices, kaya kumpirmahin mo ang current rates sa site ng bawat vendor bago bumili.

Script outline

- **Hook (0:00–0:30):** Show a blurry clip next to an upscaled one. Say: "I tested four tools to find the cheapest way to get this result."
- **Set the rules:** Explain that you used the same test clips (low-light, fast motion, old 720p footage) and the same export settings in every tool.
- **Pricing models explained:** Credits (Runway) vs. desktop software (Topaz) vs. creative enhancer (Magnific) vs. editor-based (CapCut).
- **Runway breakdown:** Put the plan table on screen: Free $0 (125 credits), Standard $12 (625), Pro $28 (2,250), Max $76 (9,500). Show the cost-per-credit math: about $0.019, $0.012 and $0.008.
- **Runway demo:** Process a clip, show how many credits it used, and estimate how many clips each plan covers.
- **Topaz demo:** Show local processing, speed, and available settings. Mention checking current licensing on their site.
- **Magnific demo:** Show the creative detail enhancement and the risk of over-sharpening or changing faces and textures.
- **CapCut demo:** Show the one-click enhance inside an edit and where it is limited.
- **Side-by-side comparison:** Split screen with zoomed crops on faces, text and fine textures. Let the viewer vote in the comments.
- **Cost scenarios:** Use three personas: casual poster, weekly YouTuber, archival or batch user. Name a pick for each.
- **Verdict:** Give the cheapest for beginners, best quality per dollar, and best for heavy use.
- **CTA:** Ask which tool to test next, point to the pricing links in the description, and remind viewers to verify current prices.
