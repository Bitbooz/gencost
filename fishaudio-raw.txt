Frequently Asked Questions
How many credits does each minute of generation cost?
Each minute of generation costs roughly 600 to 625 credits.
What's the difference between Free and Premium plans?
Free plan gives you free generations per month while Premium provides credits for up to 200 minutes of highest quality speech generation.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?
Yes, you can switch between monthly and yearly billing at any time. When switching to yearly, you'll save at least 25% compared to monthly billing. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), debit cards, and various regional payment methods through our secure payment processor.
When do my credits reset?
Credits get replenished and reset each month. See your remaining credits at
text to speech
page.
What happens to my unused minutes?
Monthly quotas reset at the beginning of each billing cycle. Unused minutes do not roll over to the next month. We recommend using your allocated minutes within your billing period.
Do you offer API access?
Yes, and you don't need a subscription for it. The API is billed separately from subscription plans, with pay-as-you-go API credit, and the S2.1 Pro Free model can be called without any credit. You can find documentation and pricing on our
developers page
.
Can I use generated voices for commercial purposes?
Premium subscribers can use verified voices (that you own) for commercial purposes. Free plan users can only use generated content for personal, non-commercial projects. Please review our terms of service for detailed usage rights.
