PixVerse Pricing

AI Video Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

In short: PixVerse costs from $10 a month (Standard), or $8 a month if you pay yearly. The biggest plan, Ultra, is $249 a month. There is no free plan. Checked 2026-11-09 on the official pricing page.

About PixVerse

PixVerse is an AI video generator with its own models, known for fast generation, effects templates and output up to 4K on higher plans.

Who it's for

Quick social clips, effects and templates at a low price.

Free plan

You get free daily credits; they renew every day at midnight UTC and expire at the end of the day if unused.

How the credits work

Plan credits refresh every 30 days from the date you subscribed, and unused ones expire when the new cycle starts. Paid plans are watermark-free and ad-free.

How much does PixVerse cost?
