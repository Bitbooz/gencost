AI Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

About Pika

Pika is an AI video app with its own Pika 2.5 and Pika H3 Flex models, plus other models such as Seedance 2.5 and Kling, all on one credit balance.

Who it's for

Short, fun social clips and quick ideas, at a low entry price.

Free plan

The Free plan has no watermark, but no commercial license; you can buy credit packs. A commercial license comes with Starter and up.

How the credits work

A 5-second Pika 2.5 clip at 720p is 20 credits; the same length with Seedance 2.5 at 720p is about 122. Creator and Fancy include free Pika H3 Flex generations every day. Plan credits don't roll over; bought top-ups last 365 days. Payments are non-refundable.

Pika pricing FAQ

How much does Pika cost?

The cheapest paid Pika plan is Starter at $10 a month ($8 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Fancy at $95 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-09.

Does Pika have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan has no watermark, but no commercial license; you can buy credit packs. A commercial license comes with Starter and up.

How much does one video clip cost on Pika?

On the Starter plan, one 5-second clip with Pika 2.5, 720p costs about $0.33 (20 credits). We work this out from Pika's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $1.00 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Pika?
