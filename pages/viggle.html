About Viggle

Viggle animates characters: you swap a person or character into a video, copy dance moves and make meme videos, even in real time.

Who it's for

Meme, dance and character-swap videos for social media.

Free plan

The Free plan has credits that refresh daily, one video at a time, watermarked Mix videos and 7-day storage.

How the credits work

Each feature uses a different number of credits depending on the model and output. Monthly plan credits expire on your billing date; bought credits don't expire but only work while you have a plan.

Viggle pricing FAQ

How much does Viggle cost?

The cheapest paid Viggle plan is Pro at $9.99 a month ($7.99 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Max at $79.99 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-13.

Does Viggle have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan has credits that refresh daily, one video at a time, watermarked Mix videos and 7-day storage.

How much does one video clip cost on Viggle?

On the Pro plan, one 10-second clip with character swap, 1 character costs about $1.25 (10 credits). We work this out from Viggle's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $3.75 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Viggle?

Free Mix videos are watermarked. Monthly credits expire on your billing date. Bought credits only work while you have a paid plan.
