About CapCut

CapCut is ByteDance's video editor for desktop, web and phone, with templates, captions and AI tools, including AI video generation with ByteDance's Seedance models.

Who it's for

Editing social videos with captions and effects, plus some AI video generation in the same app.

Free plan

The Free plan includes basic editing tools and free templates.

How the credits work

Pro includes 1,200 AI credits a month, enough for up to 240 seconds of 720p video with Seedance 2.0 Mini (about 5 credits per second). The per-second Seedance 2.5 prices on CapCut's page are first-month offers.

CapCut pricing FAQ

How much does CapCut cost?

The cheapest paid CapCut plan is Pro at $19.99 a month ($15 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Ultra at $79 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does CapCut have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan includes basic editing tools and free templates.

How much does one video clip cost on CapCut?

On the Pro plan, one 10-second clip with Seedance 2.0 Mini, 720p costs about $0.83 (50 credits). We work this out from CapCut's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $2.50 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with CapCut?

The AI video per-second prices shown on CapCut's page are first-month offers. Renews automatically.
