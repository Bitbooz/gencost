About Adobe Firefly

Adobe Firefly is Adobe's AI image and video generator, built into Photoshop, Express and the Firefly web app.

Who it's for

Commercially safe images for people who already work in Adobe apps.

Free plan

The free plan gives a few generations a day that refresh daily.

How the credits work

Paid plans give generative credits each month. They reset on your billing date and do not roll over.
