A real-dollar breakdown of Kling, Runway, Pika, and Luma pricing — plus which free tiers are actually usable in 2026.

Quick Answer

If you just want the cheapest usable paid plan, Kling's Standard tier (~$6.99–$10/mo equivalent) and Pika's Standard plan ($10/mo) currently offer the lowest cost-per-generated-second among mainstream AI video generators. Luma's Lite plan and Runway's Standard plan sit a tier above in price but offer better motion consistency. Free tiers across the board remain heavily watermarked or capped at 3–5 generations/day.

Why it's cheap
