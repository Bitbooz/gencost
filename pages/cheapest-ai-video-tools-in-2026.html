Kling AI — Best Overall Value

Kling has consistently undercut Runway and Pika on cost-per-generation since its global rollout, and 2026 pricing keeps that trend going. The Standard plan at $10/month gives you 660 credits monthly, enough for roughly 60–90 standard 5-second clips depending on mode (Standard vs Professional).
