Best Runway Alternatives in 2026: Cheaper AI Video per Clip

Runway is one of the best-known AI video tools, but also one of the most expensive per clip. On its $15 Standard plan, a 5-second Gen-4.5 clip uses 60 of the plan's 625 credits, about $1.44. These are the alternatives that make a clip for less, on their cheapest paid plan.

Quick answer
Cheapest big-name alternatives: Luma Plus (Ray3.2), about $0.30 per 5-second 720p clip, and Pika Starter (Pika 2.5), about $0.33.
Best value with Kling quality: Kling AI Standard, about $0.40 per 5-second Kling 3.0 clip.
Runway Standard with Gen-4.5: about $1.44 per 5-second clip, three to five times as much.

Which alternative fits you

Luma: closest to Runway, at a fifth of the price

Luma's Ray3.2 is a cinematic model like Runway's, with clear per-second pricing and commercial use on every plan. Its $30 Plus plan is twice Runway Standard's price, but covers about eight minutes of 720p video a month instead of under one.

Pika and Vidu: cheapest for short social clips

Pika Starter and Vidu Standard both cost $10. Pika 2.5 makes 720p clips; Vidu Q4 is even cheaper per clip but makes 540p video, which is fine for phones.

Kling: best quality for the money

Kling 3.0 costs about $0.40 per 5-second clip on the $8.80 Standard plan, and Kling 4.0, with sound, about $0.67. See Runway vs Kling for a closer look.

When Runway is still worth it

Runway's strength is editing: Aleph 2.0 can change an existing video, and Runway hosts other models such as Kling 4.0 and Seedance next to its own. On Max, up to one month of unused credits rolls over. If you need those tools, the higher price per clip can pay off.

Watch out

  • Retries cost the same as keepers. If you need three tries per clip, multiply each price by three.
  • Resolution and sound change the price. 1080p usually costs about 1.5 to 4 times as much as 720p.
  • Commercial use. Check the plan; see cheapest AI video for commercial use.
