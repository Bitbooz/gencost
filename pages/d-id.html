AI Video Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

About D-ID

D-ID makes talking-avatar and talking-photo videos: you upload a photo or pick an avatar, add a script, and the avatar speaks it.

Who it's for

Short presenter videos and animating portraits.

Free plan

There is a trial with a full-screen watermark, for personal use only.

How the credits work

The watermark and license depend on the plan: Lite has a D-ID watermark and is for personal use only; Pro adds a commercial license with a small AI watermark; Advanced lets you use your own logo.

D-ID pricing FAQ

How much does D-ID cost?

The cheapest paid D-ID plan is Lite at $4.70 a month ($4.67 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Advanced at $108 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Does D-ID have a free plan?

Yes. The Trial plan costs $0 and includes 3 minutes. There is a trial with a full-screen watermark, for personal use only.

Are there hidden costs with D-ID?

Lite is for personal use only and has a D-ID watermark. Commercial use starts on Pro.
