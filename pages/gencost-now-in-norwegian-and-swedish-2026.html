GenCost Now in Norwegian and Swedish: 21 Languages

Quick answer
GenCost is now available in Norwegian at getgencost.com/no/ and Swedish at getgencost.com/sv/.
Every tool page, comparison table, the calculator and the clip finder are translated, with the same prices as the English site, read from the official pricing pages every morning.
That makes 21 languages. Hei og velkommen! Hej och välkommen!
Last checked: 2026-11-16

GenCost started in English and has grown one language at a time. With Norwegian and Swedish, people in Scandinavia can now compare the real cost of AI video, image, voice and music tools in their own language.

What you get in Norwegian and Swedish

  • All the tool pages: Kling, Runway, HeyGen, ElevenLabs, Suno, Midjourney and 40+ more, with every plan and what it includes.
  • The comparison tables for video, image, voice and music, sorted by the cheapest paid plan.
  • The calculator and the cheapest clip finder, to see what one video clip really costs on each platform.
  • The articles and guides, from free plans to paying yearly instead of monthly.

Updated every morning, in every language

The translated pages are not copies made once. Every morning GenCost reads the official pricing pages, updates the English site, and then rebuilds every page in all 21 languages. A price that changes on Monday is correct in Norwegian and Swedish on Monday too.

Prices are in US dollars

Almost every AI tool sells its plans in US dollars, so GenCost shows the prices in dollars, before tax, exactly as the official pages list them. That makes the tools easy to compare with each other. When you pay from Norway or Sweden, your card converts the price to kroner, and VAT is usually added at checkout.
