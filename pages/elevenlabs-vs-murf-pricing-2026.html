ElevenLabs vs Murf: Cost per Minute of AI Voice (2026)

ElevenLabs sells credits, Murf sells hours of voice per year, so their plans are hard to compare. We turned both into one number: what one minute of finished voiceover costs.

Quick answer
Per minute they are almost the same: about 14 to 17 cents on both.
Cheapest start: ElevenLabs Starter, $6 a month (and $1 for the first month), with commercial use.
For business use, Murf needs its Business plan at $66 a month; ElevenLabs allows commercial use from Starter.

What the numbers mean

Same price per minute

Once you use your whole plan, both tools land at about 14 to 17 cents per minute of voice. Price alone won't decide it, so choose by voices, languages and how you work.

ElevenLabs is cheaper to start

ElevenLabs Starter costs $6 a month and only $1 for the first month, and includes commercial use. Its Free plan gives 10,000 credits a month, but without a commercial license.

Murf charges more for business rights

Murf's Creator plan has no business license; that only comes with Business at $66 a month. Its Free plan has 10 minutes of voice but no downloads.

Watch out

  • Shared credits. On ElevenLabs, all tools (voice, dubbing, sound effects) use one credit pool, so other features reduce your minutes.
  • Yearly hours. Murf's hours are counted per year. Use them up early and you wait for the new year or upgrade.
  • First-month prices. ElevenLabs Creator is half price only for the first month.
