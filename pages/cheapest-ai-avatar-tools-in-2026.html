Cheapest AI Avatar Tools in 2026: HeyGen vs Synthesia vs Free Tiers

AI Tools Pricing Breakdown · GenCost Analysis

AI avatar video tools let you turn a script into a talking presenter without a camera, studio, or actor. The catch: pricing varies wildly between providers, and "free" tiers often hide watermark requirements, minute caps, or resolution limits that make them unusable for real work. Here's what HeyGen, Synthesia, and the leading free options actually cost in 2026.

Quick Answer

Cheapest paid option: HeyGen's Creator plan at $29/month beats Synthesia's entry plan on cost-per-minute. Best free tier: HeyGen also wins here, offering more free minutes per month than Synthesia's free plan, though both apply watermarks on free-tier exports.

HeyGen Pricing Breakdown

Free Plan

$0/month — 3 videos per month, each capped at 1 minute, HeyGen watermark included, access to a limited avatar library. Good for testing the tool, not for publishable content.

Creator Plan — $29/mo

15 credits per month (roughly 15 minutes of avatar video), no watermark, access to most stock avatars, 720p/1080p export, and basic voice cloning. This is the cheapest practical entry point for watermark-free HeyGen video.

Team Plan — starts around $39–89/mo

Multiple seats, more monthly credits, custom avatar creation, and brand kit features. Pricing scales with seat count and credit volume.

Enterprise

Custom quote. Includes API access, unlimited avatars, SSO, and dedicated support. Typically starts in the thousands per month depending on volume.

Synthesia Pricing Breakdown

Free Plan

$0/month — limited to 3 videos total (not monthly), roughly 36 minutes per year, watermark included, access to a small subset of stock avatars. More restrictive than HeyGen's free tier.

Starter Plan — $29/mo

10 minutes of video per month, no watermark, access to 10+ stock avatars, 10+ languages, and basic screen recording. Billed annually for this rate; monthly
