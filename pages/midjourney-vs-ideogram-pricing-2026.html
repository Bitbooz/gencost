Midjourney and Ideogram both start around $10 to $20 a month, but they sell very different things: Midjourney sells GPU time, Ideogram sells credits plus an unlimited slow queue. Here is what each plan really gets you, in US dollars.

Quick answer
Cheapest start: Midjourney Basic at $10 a month, but with no unlimited mode and public images.
Cheapest unlimited: Ideogram Plus at $20 ($15 paid yearly), against $30 for Midjourney Standard.

Which should you pick?

Pick Midjourney if image quality comes first

Midjourney's images are widely seen as the most artistic. Basic is the cheapest way in at $10, but it has no Relax mode, so you stop when the 200 fast minutes are gone. For steady use, Standard at $30 is the plan most people need.

Pick Ideogram for text, privacy and price

Ideogram Plus gives private images and an unlimited slow queue for $20, or $15 paid yearly: about half of what Midjourney charges for unlimited, and a quarter of what it charges for private images. It is also the better tool when your image needs readable text.

Watch out

  • Midjourney: images are public below Pro, and companies earning over $1M a year must use Pro or Mega.
  • Ideogram: the unlimited slow queue only covers Ideogram's own models. Partner models, image edits and upscales still use credits.
  • Ideogram Free: free credits only if you sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft.
