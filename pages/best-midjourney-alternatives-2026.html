Which alternative should you pick?

Adobe Firefly: cheapest, and safe for commercial work

Firefly Standard costs $9.99 a month for 2,000 generative credits. Adobe trains its own Firefly models on licensed content and says their images can be used commercially, which matters for client work. Credits reset each month.

Recraft: private images and vector art from $10

Recraft Basic ($12, or $10 a month paid yearly) gives 1,000 credits, private images, commercial rights and access to other models such as Flux and GPT Image. It is the best pick for logos, icons and anything you need as SVG.

Leonardo: private images and consistent styles

Leonardo Essential ($12, or $9.60 paid yearly) gives 8,500 tokens a month and private images. Unused tokens build up in a bank, up to a cap, so a quiet month isn't wasted.

Ideogram: the closest thing to Midjourney Standard

Midjourney's unlimited Relax mode starts at $30 a month. Ideogram Plus costs $20 ($15 paid yearly) and, once your 2,400 monthly credits run out, lets you keep making images with Ideogram's own models in a slower queue with no limit. Your images stay private, and Ideogram is known for clean text inside images.

OpenArt and Freepik: many models in one account

OpenArt and Freepik both give you many image models, including some Midjourney-style ones, under one subscription. OpenArt Starter includes no commercial use rights; Freepik's credits are given per year.

When Midjourney is still worth it

If image quality and style matter more than anything else, Midjourney Standard at $30 a month with unlimited Relax mode is still hard to beat for volume. Just remember that your images are public below Pro.

Watch out

  • Free plans make public images on Ideogram, Recraft and Leonardo.
  • Recraft: commercial rights only while you subscribe.
  • OpenArt: no commercial use on Starter.
  • Midjourney: companies earning over $1M a year must use Pro or Mega.
