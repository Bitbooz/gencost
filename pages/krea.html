About Krea

Krea is a creative suite with real-time image generation, its own Krea 3 model and many third-party video models such as Kling, Veo and Seedance, plus upscaling and an agent that plans work for you.

Who it's for

Designers who mix image and video work and want many models in one app.

Free plan

The Free plan gives 100 compute units a day, but no commercial license.

How the credits work

Everything uses compute units. Krea's own table shows about 19 five-second Kling 2.5 videos on the Basic plan's 5,000 units. Monthly units don't roll over; bought units expire after 90 days.

Krea pricing FAQ

How much does Krea cost?

The cheapest paid Krea plan is Basic at $9 a month ($5 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Max at $105 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does Krea have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 100 units/day. The Free plan gives 100 compute units a day, but no commercial license.

How much does one video clip cost on Krea?

On the Basic plan, one 5-second clip with Kling 2.5 costs about $0.47 (263 credits). We work this out from Krea's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $1.42 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Krea?

Free has no commercial license. Monthly units don't roll over; bought units expire after 90 days.
