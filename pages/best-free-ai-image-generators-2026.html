The free plans one by one

Leonardo: the most generous daily allowance

Leonardo gives 150 tokens every day, which is enough to try its models properly. Everything you make on the free plan is public, and Leonardo keeps more rights over free images than over paid ones.

Ideogram: best for text in images

Ideogram's 30 free credits a week only come if you sign in with Google, Apple or Microsoft; accounts made with an email and password get none. Free images are public.

Recraft: try vector art for free

Recraft's free plan lets you try its own models a few times a day per style, but images are public and you may not use them commercially. For a real logo or icon set you need Basic.

Adobe Firefly: a few free images every day

Firefly's free plan gives a small number of generations that refresh daily, which is enough to see if Firefly suits you before paying $9.99 a month.

Chatbots

ChatGPT and Google Gemini can also make images on their free plans, with daily limits that change often. We don't track them because they don't sell image plans of their own.

Watch out

  • Free usually means public: anyone can see images from the free plans of Leonardo, Ideogram and Recraft.
  • Commercial use: for client work, a logo or anything you sell, use a paid plan.
  • Midjourney has no free trial at all.
