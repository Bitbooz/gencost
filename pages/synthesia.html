About Synthesia

Synthesia makes professional videos with AI avatars and voices, aimed at companies: training, onboarding, how-to and internal communication videos.

Who it's for

Businesses that need many consistent presenter videos, in many languages, without a film crew.

Free plan

The Basic plan is free, but its videos carry a Synthesia watermark.

How the credits work

Each plan includes video minutes and credits that reset every billing period; unused minutes don't roll over. Extras cost credits, for example dubbing at 80 credits per minute with lip-sync (40 without).

Synthesia pricing FAQ

How much does Synthesia cost?

The cheapest paid Synthesia plan is Starter at $19 a month. The biggest plan we track is Pro at $89 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Does Synthesia have a free plan?

Yes. The Basic plan costs $0 and includes 500 credits. The Basic plan is free, but its videos carry a Synthesia watermark.
