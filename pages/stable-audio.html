About Stable Audio

Stable Audio is Stability AI's music and sound generator; it makes full mixes or separate tracks up to 6 minutes long.

Who it's for

Background music, sound beds and multi-track stems.

Free plan

The Free plan gives about 12 full-mix generations a month, and includes a commercial license.
