Best Free Tiers for Testing

  • Cartesia offers the most generous free character allowance at 20,000/month, good for prototyping.
  • ElevenLabs free tier is smaller (10,000 chars) but includes access to its most natural-sounding voices for evaluation.
  • PlayHT free tier (12,500 chars) is solid for short-form content testing but adds watermarks on some voices.
  • Murf free trial is the most limited, a one-time 10-minute allowance rather than a recurring monthly quota.

Which Tool Is Actually Cheapest?

If your priority is pure cost-per-character, Cartesia currently wins at roughly $0.50 per 10,000 characters on its $5 plan. ElevenLabs is a close second at the same price point but with fewer characters included. PlayHT becomes cost-effective only at higher volumes due to its annual billing discounts. Murf is the most expensive per-character option but justifies its price with built-in video and voiceover editing tools that the others don't offer.

For most small projects and solo creators, starting with a free tier (Cartesia or ElevenLabs) and upgrading only once you hit real usage limits is the most cost-efficient strategy in 2026.
