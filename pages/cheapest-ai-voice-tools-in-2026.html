Quick Answer

If you just need a few minutes of narration a month, Cartesia's free tier and PlayHT's free plan are the cheapest realistic options. For paid plans scaling beyond hobby use, ElevenLabs' Creator tier ($22/mo) remains the best value-per-minute for quality voice cloning, while PlayHT wins on raw character volume per dollar at higher tiers.

Murf is the most expensive per-minute option of the four, but it bundles video sync and studio tools that the others don't — so it's not a fair apples-to-apples price comparison unless you need that workflow.

ElevenLabs remains the benchmark for voice quality and emotional range. Its free tier gives roughly 10 minutes of generation monthly — enough for testing but not production use.

Cartesia

Cartesia's Sonic model is built for speed and low latency, which makes it popular for real-time voice agents. It's also currently the cheapest per-minute option among the major players.

PlayHT prices by character count rather than minutes, which rewards longer-form content creators. Its Creator plan offers one of the best character-to-dollar ratios available in 2026.
