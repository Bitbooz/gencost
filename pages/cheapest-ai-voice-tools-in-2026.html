Quick Verdict

If you just need occasional narration, free tiers from ElevenLabs or PlayHT will cover most hobby projects. For high-volume production use, Cartesia currently offers the lowest cost-per-character among premium-quality voice models, while Murf remains the cheapest option for straightforward business voiceovers without API complexity.

Tool-by-Tool Breakdown

ElevenLabs Best Free Tier

ElevenLabs remains the most recognizable name in AI voice, known for natural-sounding cloned voices and multilingual support. The free tier gives 10,000 characters/month — enough for short-form content or testing voice clones before committing.

  • Free: 10,000 chars/mo, limited voice cloning
  • Starter: $5/mo — 30,000 chars, instant voice cloning
  • Creator: $22/mo — 100,000 chars, higher quality model access
  • API pricing scales from ~$0.15–$0.30 per 1,000 chars depending on plan tier
Best for: creators who want highest voice realism and don't mind paying a premium per character.
