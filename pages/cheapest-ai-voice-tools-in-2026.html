Cheapest AI Voice Tools in 2026

AI voice generation has become essential for creators, developers, and businesses producing voiceovers, audiobooks, and conversational AI agents. But pricing models vary wildly between providers — some charge per character, some per minute of audio, and some bundle usage into monthly credits. Below we break down the real-dollar costs of the most popular AI voice tools in 2026, including their free tiers.

Free Tier Breakdown

  • ElevenLabs: 10,000 characters/month free, includes limited voice cloning and standard voices.
  • Cartesia: One-time free trial credits for testing; no recurring monthly free tier as of 2026.
  • PlayHT: 12,500 characters/month free, decent for small projects or testing voice quality.
  • Murf: 10 minutes of free audio generation, access to a limited voice library.

Which Tool Is Cheapest Overall?

For pure cost-per-character efficiency, Cartesia currently offers the lowest rate at scale, making it attractive for developers building high-volume voice applications. PlayHT is a strong runner-up thanks to its generous character allowance on the Creator plan. ElevenLabs remains the most popular due to voice quality and cloning features, but costs more per character at lower tiers. Murf is the most expensive on a per-character basis since it targets professional voiceover and video use cases rather than high-volume API usage.
