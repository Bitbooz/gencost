Which Tool Is Cheapest Overall?

For high-volume, low-cost narration, PlayHT consistently offers the lowest cost per minute of generated audio. If you need the most natural-sounding voices with flexible API access at a low entry price, ElevenLabs' $5/mo Starter plan is hard to beat for small projects. Cartesia is best suited for developers building real-time, low-latency voice applications, though it can get expensive at scale. Murf remains the priciest per minute but includes extras like video dubbing and team collaboration features that may justify the cost for business users.

Tips to Minimize Costs

  • Use free tiers for testing before committing to a paid plan.
  • Batch your text-to-speech requests to avoid hitting character limits with inefficient short calls.
  • Compare annual vs. monthly billing — most tools offer 15-20% discounts annually.
  • Watch for overage fees; they can make "cheap" plans expensive fast at high volume.
  • If building an app, consider Cartesia or ElevenLabs' API pricing instead of subscription tiers for more predictable scaling costs.
