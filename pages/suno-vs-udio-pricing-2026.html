Suno vs Udio Pricing 2026: The Full Cost Breakdown

Both platforms turn text prompts into full songs in under a minute. But their pricing models work very differently once you factor in credits, generations per song, and commercial usage rights. Here's what each song actually costs in 2026.

Quick Summary

SUNO

Pricing Model

Monthly/annual subscription with credit allowances. Credits are spent per generation (2 clips per generation).

$10–$30/month

Pro and Premier tiers, billed monthly or annually with a discount.

UDIO

Pricing Model

Monthly/annual subscription with credit allowances. Credits spent per generation (2 clips per generation, similar structure).

$10–$30/month

Standard and Pro tiers, billed monthly or annually with a discount.

Suno Pricing Plans (2026)

Plan Monthly Price Annual Price (per mo) Credits / Month Commercial Use
Free $0 — 50 credits (~10 songs) No

Each "song" generation produces 2 clip variations and costs roughly 5 credits. Unused credits roll over up to a cap on paid plans.

Udio Pricing Plans (2026)

Plan Monthly Price Annual Price (per mo) Credits / Month Commercial Use
Free $0 — 1,200 credits (~10 songs) No
Standard $10 $8 12,000 credits (~400 songs) Yes
Pro $30 $24 48,000 credits (~1,600 songs) Yes, high-quality downloads

Udio's credit numbers look larger but each generation consumes more credits (~30 per generation), so effective song output is comparable to Suno at similar price points.
