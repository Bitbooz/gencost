Suno vs Udio Pricing 2026: Which AI Music Generator Costs Less Per Song?

Both platforms sell "credits," but the real cost per usable track differs more than the sticker price suggests. Here's the full 2026 breakdown — plans, overage costs, commercial rights, and the actual price per minute of music.

Updated for 2026 Real-Dollar Pricing Music AI

Quick Summary

Suno is generally cheaper per song for casual and hobbyist use thanks to a generous free tier and lower-cost Pro plan. Udio is priced similarly on paper but produces fewer total generations per dollar at the entry tier, though its higher audio fidelity on paid plans can justify the cost for producers who need fewer retries to land a usable take.

If you're optimizing purely for cost per minute of finished music, Suno currently wins at every tier except the top-end "Ultra" plans, where pricing converges.
