About Ideogram

Ideogram is an AI image generator that is especially good at readable text inside images, such as logos, posters and thumbnails.

Who it's for

Posters, thumbnails, logos and any image with text in it.

Free plan

There is a free plan, but its images are public. Private images start on the Plus plan.

How the credits work

Monthly credits don't roll over; credits you buy separately last 365 days and are used after your monthly credits.

Ideogram pricing FAQ

How much does Ideogram cost?

The cheapest paid Ideogram plan is Plus at $20 a month ($15 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Pro at $60 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does Ideogram have a free plan?

There is a free plan, but its images are public. Private images start on the Plus plan.

Are there hidden costs with Ideogram?

Free images are public; private images from the Plus plan. Monthly credits don't roll over; bought credits last 365 days.
