About Elyum

Elyum is an AI video and image platform that gives you many top models, including Kling 4.0, Kling 3, Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3, under one plan.

Who it's for

Trying several leading video models from one subscription, starting at $9 a month.

Free plan

How the credits work

Each plan gives credits every month (Plus 900, Pro 3,500, Ultra 9,000). Kling 4.0 uses 17 credits per second at 720p and 25 at 1080p, with sound included.

Elyum pricing FAQ

How much does Elyum cost?

The cheapest paid Elyum plan is Plus at $9 a month. The biggest plan we track is Ultra at $69 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-14.

Does Elyum have a free plan?

New accounts may get up to 100 welcome credits once, but they can't be used on Kling 4.0.

How much does one video clip cost on Elyum?

On the Plus plan, one 10-second clip with Kling 4.0, 720p costs about $1.70 (170 credits). We work this out from Elyum's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $5.10 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Elyum?

The free welcome credits can't be used on Kling 4.0. Yearly billing saves up to 17%, but the pricing page shows no yearly prices.
