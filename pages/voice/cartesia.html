About Cartesia

Cartesia makes very fast, low-latency AI voices (Sonic) and speech to text (Ink), built for developers and real-time voice agents.

Who it's for

Apps and voice agents, and anyone who wants the most speech for the money.

Free plan

The Free plan includes 20,000 credits a month, but no commercial license; that starts on Pro.

How the credits work

Speech costs 1 credit per character, about 750 to 800 credits per minute of audio. Unused credits roll over, up to twice your monthly amount.
