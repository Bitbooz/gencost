About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is the best-known AI voice platform: text to speech, voice cloning, dubbing, sound effects and music, with a large library of voices in many languages.

Who it's for

High-quality narration, voice cloning and dubbing, especially if you use several of its tools.

Free plan

The Free plan includes 10,000 credits a month, but no commercial license; that starts on Starter.

How the credits work

All tools share one monthly credit pool. Text to speech costs about 1 credit per character, so 30,000 credits is roughly 30 to 40 minutes of speech. Starter is $1 and Creator $11 for the first month.
