About Fliki

Fliki turns text, blog posts and slides into videos with AI voices, stock media and avatars, in 80+ languages.

Who it's for

Faceless YouTube videos, explainers and social posts from text.

Free plan

The Free plan gives about 5 minutes of audio and video a month, at 720p with a Fliki watermark.

How the credits work

Credits are roughly minutes of content: Standard's 180 credits make about 60 to 90 minutes of video. Paid plans remove the watermark and include commercial rights.

Fliki pricing FAQ

How much does Fliki cost?

The cheapest paid Fliki plan is Standard at $39 a month. The biggest plan we track is Premium at $119 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does Fliki have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 3 credits. The Free plan gives about 5 minutes of audio and video a month, at 720p with a Fliki watermark.

Are there hidden costs with Fliki?

Free videos have a Fliki watermark and are 720p. Commercial rights only on paid plans.
