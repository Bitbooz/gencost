About Descript

Descript is an audio and video editor where you edit by editing the text transcript, with AI tools for voices, filler-word removal and studio sound.

Who it's for

Podcasters and YouTubers who edit a lot of talking content.

Free plan

There is a free plan to try text-based editing and the AI tools. Watermark-free export needs a paid plan.

How the credits work

Plans include media hours (how much audio and video you can upload and edit) and AI credits for the AI tools.
