Speechify pricing FAQ

How much does Speechify cost?

The cheapest paid Speechify plan is Premium at $29 a month ($11.58 a month if you pay yearly). Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Does Speechify have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan has 10 basic voices at up to 1.5x speed.

Is Speechify cheaper if you pay yearly?

Yes, much cheaper. Premium costs $29 a month when you pay monthly, but $11.58 a month when you pay for a full year ($139 a year). That is about 60% less.

What do you get with Speechify Premium?

Premium unlocks 1,000+ natural voices in 60+ languages, listening at up to 5x speed, Scan & Listen for printed text, AI summaries and chat, voice typing, AI podcasts, and links to Google Drive, Dropbox and OneDrive.

What can you do with Speechify for free?

The Free plan reads text aloud with 10 basic voices at up to 1.5x speed. It covers text to speech only, without the AI features.

Is Speechify Studio included in Premium?

No. Speechify Studio (voice-overs and dubbing) and the Speechify API are separate products with their own pricing. Premium covers the reading app.
