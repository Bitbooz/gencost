What happened to PlayHT?

Meta took over the PlayHT team in mid-2025. The PlayHT API stopped working in July 2025, and the platform closed for good on December 31, 2025. Cloned voices and audio files were not moved anywhere, so if you used a PlayHT voice clone you need to clone your voice again in a new tool. Because PlayHT no longer sells plans, it has been removed from GenCost's price lists.

Which PlayHT alternative should you pick?

Cartesia: cheapest, with commercial use from $5

Cartesia's Pro plan is the cheapest paid voice plan we track. It includes a commercial license and instant voice cloning, and unused credits roll over up to twice your monthly amount.

ElevenLabs: the most complete PlayHT replacement

ElevenLabs has the widest set of tools: voice cloning, a studio for long projects, dubbing and sound effects. Starter is $1 for the first month, then $6. Professional voice cloning starts on Creator, which is $11 for the first month and then $22.

Murf AI: best for regular voice-overs

Murf gives you a large yearly allowance of voice time and an editor that lines speech up with video. Its Free plan can't download audio and has no commercial rights.

Moving from PlayHT

  • Re-clone your voice. Your PlayHT clones are gone. Record 1–3 minutes of clean audio and clone it again in ElevenLabs or Cartesia.
  • Update your apps. If you used the PlayHT API, ElevenLabs and Cartesia both have text-to-speech APIs you can switch to.
  • Check the license. The free plans of ElevenLabs, Cartesia and Murf don't allow commercial use.
