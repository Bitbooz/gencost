The AI Price Exchange: AI Tools Priced Like Shares

Quick answer
The AI price exchange shows every AI tool GenCost tracks like a share, with a ticker, charts and a daily index. The GenCost AI Price Index (GCX) started at 1000 on 2026-11-08 and stood at 969.4 on 2026-11-19: AI tools got 3.1% cheaper, and no tool raised its price.
Last checked: 2026-11-19

AI tools change their prices quietly: a yearly discount here, a cheaper plan there. On a normal pricing page you never see the change. On the AI price exchange you do, the same way you see a share go up or down.

What's on the exchange

  • A ticker tape running across the screen, like on Wall Street, with every tool's symbol, price and change.
  • GCX, the GenCost AI Price Index: one number for what AI tools cost, starting at 1000.
  • Sector indices for video and avatar, image, voice and music.
  • The biggest price drops and rises, and a chart for every tool.
  • All 50 "listings" with 1-day, 7-day and since-start changes. Green ▼ means cheaper, good news for buyers.

How it works

Every morning GenCost reads the official pricing page of each tool. A new price only counts after it has been seen on two different days, so a misreading or a one-day banner never moves the index. Each tool starts at 100; GCX is the average of all tools, times ten. AI prices don't move every day like shares, so flat lines are real data too. It's not a stock and you can't buy it, but it tells you when it's a good time to buy a plan.

Questions people ask

Are AI tools getting cheaper?

So far, yes: the GCX index fell from 1000 to 969.4 between 2026-11-08 and 2026-11-19, mostly through bigger yearly discounts on video and image tools.

How often is the AI price exchange updated?

Every morning, after GenCost has read every tool's official pricing page. A new price shows up after it has been confirmed on a second day.

Can I use the data?

Yes. The numbers behind the exchange are free to use with credit (CC BY 4.0): getgencost.com/data/exchange.json and the daily dataset on Hugging Face (Bitbooz/ai-tool-prices).

GenCost has no affiliate links and no paid placements. Prices are read from each tool's official pricing page every morning.
