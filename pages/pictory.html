Pictory Pricing

AI Video Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

In short: Pictory costs from $29 a month (Starter), or $25 a month if you pay yearly. The biggest plan, Teams, is $199 a month. There is a free option with limited use (see below). Checked 2026-11-11 on the official pricing page.

Plans

Starter: $29/mo · 2,000 credits
Professional: $59/mo · 6,000 credits
Teams: $199/mo · 18,000 credits

About Pictory

Pictory turns scripts, blog posts, URLs and long recordings into short videos with stock footage, AI voices and captions.

Who it's for

Marketers and creators who turn text or long videos into short social clips.

Free plan

A free trial gives 300 credits, up to 3 videos of 5 minutes each.

How the credits work

Credits are shared across all AI features. Annual plans give a full year of credits upfront (24,000 on Starter); monthly plans give them each month (2,000).

Pictory pricing FAQ

How much does Pictory cost?

The cheapest paid Pictory plan is Starter at $29 a month ($25 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Teams at $199 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Does Pictory have a free plan?

A free trial gives 300 credits, up to 3 videos of 5 minutes each.

Are there hidden costs with Pictory?

Yearly plans: a year of credits upfront, monthly plans: credits each month. Free trial: 3 videos, max 5 minutes.
