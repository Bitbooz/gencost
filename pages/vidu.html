About Vidu

Vidu is ShengShu's AI video generator, with its Q4 model and support for 3 to 16-second clips and reference images.

Who it's for

Cheap drafts, social clips and testing ideas before rendering them in a pricier tool.

Free plan

There is a free plan. Commercial use and watermark-free downloads need a paid plan.

How the credits work

The Q4 model at 540p uses 2 credits per second, so a 10-second clip is about 20 credits. Paid plans also include off-peak generations.

Vidu pricing FAQ

How much does Vidu cost?

The cheapest paid Vidu plan is Standard at $10 a month ($8.25 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Ultimate at $230 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-09.

Does Vidu have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. There is a free plan. Commercial use and watermark-free downloads need a paid plan.

How much does one video clip cost on Vidu?

On the Standard plan, one 8-second clip with Q4, 540p costs about $0.20 (16 credits). We work this out from Vidu's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $0.60 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Vidu?

Commercial use and watermark-free downloads only on paid plans.
