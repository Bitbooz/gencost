Higgsfield

Official pricing page →

Higgsfield focuses on high-quality cinematic generative video using advanced models such as Kling and Seedance. It is better suited for creative text-to-video and image-to-video work than traditional talking avatars. Yearly plans significantly improve the cost per credit.

Key Features

  • Access to Kling and Seedance video models
  • Strong cinematic and generative video quality
  • Text-to-video and image-to-video
  • Better value on annual billing
  • Good for stylized and high-production-looking clips
