About Higgsfield

Higgsfield is a creative platform that gives you many top video and image models in one place, including Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.5 and Nano Banana Pro, with presets for cinematic shots and viral effects.

Who it's for

Creators who want to try several leading models without paying for each one separately.

Free plan

There is a free option with limited use, so you can test it before paying.

How the credits work

Higgsfield estimates about 14 credits per Kling 3.0 video and 2 credits per image. Its pricing page also lists how many videos of each model a plan covers.

Higgsfield pricing FAQ

How much does Higgsfield cost?

The cheapest paid Higgsfield plan is Starter at $19 a month. The biggest plan we track is Ultra at $129 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

Does Higgsfield have a free plan?

There is a free option with limited use, so you can test it before paying.

How much does one video clip cost on Higgsfield?

On the Starter plan, one 5-second clip with Kling 3.0 costs about $0.99 (14 credits). We work this out from Higgsfield's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more.
