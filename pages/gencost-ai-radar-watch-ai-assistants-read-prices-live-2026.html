The AI Radar: Watch AI Assistants Read GenCost Live

Quick answer
The AI radar is a live 3D globe on GenCost. Every glowing line is an AI, such as ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity or Meta AI, reading GenCost's prices right now, often while answering someone's question. It updates every 15 seconds and stores nothing about people.
Last checked: 2026-11-20

When you ask an AI assistant "what's the cheapest way to make AI videos?", it often fetches a web page to check today's prices. More and more often, that page is GenCost. Now you can watch it happen.

What you see on the radar

  • A glowing line from the AI company's home (for example San Francisco for ChatGPT and Claude, Seattle for Amazon, Moscow for Yandex, Beijing for Baidu) to the region where the page's language is spoken.
  • Cyan: an AI reading while answering someone ("a user asked").
  • Purple: an AI learning the page for later (crawling).
  • Pink: someone's AI asking GenCost's AI connector directly, for example for the cheapest option.
  • A line of text such as "ChatGPT read Kling's prices while answering someone in Portuguese, 12 seconds ago", and a list of the latest visits.

Why GenCost built it

AI assistants are becoming the place where people compare prices. GenCost is built for them: prices read from each official pricing page every morning, translated into 21 languages, and an AI connector for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and more. The radar makes that invisible traffic visible, so you can see that AI answers about AI prices come from real, checked numbers.

Honest by design

The radar shows which AI read which page, in which language. It does not show where any person is: an AI fetches pages from its own servers, so a visitor's country can't be known, and GenCost doesn't guess. Nothing personal is stored: no IP address, no cookie, only the AI's name, the page and the time.

Questions people ask

Is it really live?

Yes. Each AI visit is recorded the moment it happens, and the radar fetches the latest ones every 15 seconds. At night in Europe and the US it can be quiet for a while.

Which AI assistants read GenCost?

Among others ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Meta AI, Amazon, Apple, Google, Bing (Copilot), Yandex, Baidu, ByteDance (Doubao) and Naver.

Can I make my AI assistant use GenCost?

Yes. Add GenCost's free AI connector at https://getgencost.com/mcp to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, VS Code, Codex, Windsurf or Gemini CLI. The setup for each app is on the AI connector page.

GenCost has no affiliate links and no paid placements. Prices are read from each tool's official pricing page every morning.
