Cheapest per hour of speech: Cartesia, from $5/mo, three to four times cheaper than ElevenLabs.

Which alternative should you pick?

Cartesia: the cheapest by far

Cartesia's Pro plan costs $5 a month and includes a commercial license and instant voice cloning. Per hour of speech it is three to four times cheaper than ElevenLabs, and unused credits roll over up to twice your monthly amount.

Murf AI: built for voice-overs

Murf sells voice time by the hour, with an editor that lines speech up with video. The Business plan gives Murf's lowest cost per hour, and it is the only Murf plan with a business license.

When ElevenLabs is still worth it

ElevenLabs does more than text to speech: dubbing, music, sound effects and a large voice library all share one credit pool. If you use those, it can still be the best deal. Starter is $1 for the first month, and Creator is $11 for the first month.

Other options

Descript is an audio and video editor with AI voices, priced by media hours rather than speech. Speechify and WellSaid Labs are also on our voice price list.

Watch out

  • Free plans are not for commercial use on ElevenLabs, Cartesia or Murf.
  • ElevenLabs: unused paid credits expire if you downgrade or cancel.
  • Murf: the Free plan can't download audio.
