Sora Is Shut Down: The Cheapest Sora Alternatives in 2026

OpenAI has closed Sora. Here are the tools that replace it, with what one video clip really costs on each, using today's prices and each tool's own credits per clip.

Quick answer
Cheapest per clip: Google Veo (in Google Flow, part of Google AI Pro) and Vidu.
Best value from a dedicated video tool: Kling AI, from $8.80/mo.
Closest swap if you used Sora for realistic clips with sound: Google Veo.
Last checked: 2026-11-07

What happened to Sora?

According to OpenAI's help center, the Sora web and app experiences were discontinued on April 26, 2026, and the Sora API on September 24, 2026. OpenAI says it will permanently delete Sora data after any final export window, so if you still have videos there, export them now at sora.chatgpt.com/sunset. Bought ChatGPT/Sora credits can still be used for Codex. Because Sora no longer has a plan you can buy, it has been removed from GenCost's price lists.

Which Sora alternative should you pick?

Google Veo: cheapest per clip, closest to Sora

Veo is Google's video model, used through Google Flow and included in the Google AI plans. Like Sora 2, it makes realistic clips with sound. Per clip it is the cheapest option in this list, and the plan also includes Google's other AI tools. The catch: you buy a whole Google AI plan, not just video.

Kling AI: best value from a dedicated video tool

Kling's Standard plan is one of the cheapest dedicated video plans we track, and its Kling 3.0 model gives strong motion and quality for the price. The first month is 30% off; the price above is what you pay after that.

Vidu: lowest cost for short, simple clips

Vidu's Q4 model at 540p is very cheap per second. It is a good pick for drafts, social clips and testing ideas before you render them in a pricier tool.

Runway: most control, highest cost

Runway's Gen-4.5 costs the most per clip here, but it has the most editing and camera tools. On the Standard and Pro plans, unused credits reset every month.

Watch out when you switch

  • Credits rarely roll over. Runway Standard and Pro, Pika, InVideo and Krea reset unused credits every month.
  • Intro prices. Kling's first month is 30% off and CapCut's per-second AI prices are first-month offers.
  • Commercial use needs a paid plan on Pika, Kling, Vidu and Krea.
