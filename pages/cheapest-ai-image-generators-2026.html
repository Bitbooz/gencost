The cheapest "unlimited" plans

If you make a lot of images, an unlimited plan can beat any cost per image. Unlimited always means a slower queue or a limited set of models:

  • Ideogram Plus (from $15 a month paid yearly): when your credits run out, you can keep using Ideogram's own models in a slower queue with no limit. Partner models, edits and upscales still need credits.
  • Midjourney Standard ($30, or $24 paid yearly): unlimited images in Relax mode, which is slower than fast GPU time. Basic has no Relax mode.
  • Freepik Plus ($45, or $33.75 paid yearly): unlimited images on some models, such as Nano Banana 2 at 1K.

Which tool should you pick?

Adobe Firefly: cheapest start, commercially safe

Firefly Standard costs $9.99 a month for 2,000 generative credits, and Adobe says images from its own Firefly models can be used commercially. It is the natural choice if you already use Photoshop or Adobe Express.

Midjourney: best-looking images for $10

Midjourney Basic gives 200 minutes of fast GPU time a month. Images are public unless you pay for Pro or Mega, and companies earning over $1M a year must be on Pro or Mega.

OpenArt: most images for the money

OpenArt gives access to more than 100 image, video and audio models from one account, and its Starter plan has the lowest cost per image in this guide on simple models. Starter does not include commercial use rights: you need Plus for that.

Ideogram: best for text in images

Ideogram is known for clean text inside images, such as posters and logos. Plus keeps your images private and lets you keep generating in a slower queue after your credits run out.

Recraft: vectors, icons and logos

Recraft makes vector graphics you can edit as SVG. Basic costs $12 a month ($10 paid yearly) for 1,000 credits and adds commercial rights and private images.

Leonardo and Freepik

Leonardo focuses on consistent styles for game art and products, and unused tokens build up in a bank on paid plans. Freepik, now renamed Magnific, bundles a large stock library with many AI models, and its credits last a full year.

Watch out

  • Free plans make public images on Ideogram, Recraft and Leonardo, and Recraft's free images can't be used commercially.
  • Midjourney: private images (Stealth Mode) only on Pro and Mega.
  • OpenArt: no commercial use on Starter.
  • Freepik: credits are given per year, so heavy use early in the year can leave you with none later.
  • Monthly credits don't roll over on Firefly, Recraft or Ideogram.
