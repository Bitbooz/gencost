Emerging Tools

Smaller and less known AI tools for video, voice and music. They are often cheaper or better for specific use cases, but harder to find. Prices change often — always confirm inside the tool.

Video

Seedance

ByteDance model. Often one of the cheaper high-quality options depending on platform. Available on multiple hosts.
