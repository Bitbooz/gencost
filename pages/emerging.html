Emerging Tools

Smaller and less known AI tools for video, voice and music. These are not always the biggest names, but they can be cheaper or better for specific use cases. Prices change often — always verify on the official site.

Video / Film

Seedance (ByteDance)

Long clips (up to 30s), multi-reference consistency, native audio. Strong physics and character consistency. ~$14/mo on some hosts.

PixVerse

Multi-shot, native audio, camera control. Good for social and cinematic work. ~$8/mo (Standard, yearly).

Wan (Alibaba)

High-quality text/image-to-video. Competitive API pricing. Frequently praised for prompt adherence and value.

OpenArt

Multi-model platform (Seedance, Kling, etc.) + character tools. $14/mo Starter.

Grok Imagine Video

Fast drafts with lip-synced speech. Platform-dependent pricing.
Sound / Voice

Fish Audio

Expressive TTS, emotion tags, voice design and cloning. Strong for characters and narration. ~$15/mo (Plus).

Hume AI

Emotion-aware voices and conversational speech. Free with credits; paid from ~$3/mo.

Resemble AI

Voice cloning + watermarking/security features. Pay-as-you-go.
Music

Mureka

Full songs with vocals, stems, remix and studio. Free limited; Pro ~$8/mo (annual).

Stable Audio (Stability AI)

Instrumentals, SFX, multi-track. Free 50 credits/mo; Solo $12/mo. Commercially safer data.

Flow Music (formerly Riffusion)

Full songs, cheaper paid tier. ~$6/mo.

Treblo (formerly Sonauto)

Unlimited-style generation with free options.

Beatoven

Scene-matched background / soundtrack music. Freemium.
Quick Recommendations
Longer or more consistent cinematic clips: Seedance or PixVerse
Cheapest strong video value: Wan or PixVerse
Expressive or directed voice: Fish Audio or Hume
Full original songs with vocals: Mureka
Safe instrumental / SFX for video: Stable Audio
Multi-model testing in one place: OpenArt
