Emerging Tools

There are many smaller and less known companies building AI tools for video, voice and music. These are not always the biggest names, but they can be cheaper or better for specific use cases.

Prices change often — always verify on the official site before committing. Many have free tiers or credits for testing.

Video / Film

  • Seedance (ByteDance) — Long clips (up to 30s), multi-reference consistency, native audio. ~$14/mo on some hosts. Strong physics and character consistency.
  • PixVerse — Multi-shot, native audio, camera control. ~$8/mo (Standard, yearly). Good for social and cinematic work.
  • Wan (Alibaba) — High-quality text/image-to-video. Competitive API pricing. Frequently praised for prompt adherence and value.
  • OpenArt — Multi-model platform (Seedance, Kling, etc.) + character tools. $14/mo Starter.
  • Grok Imagine Video — Fast drafts with lip-synced speech. Platform-dependent pricing.

Sound / Voice

  • Fish Audio — Expressive TTS, emotion tags, voice design and cloning. ~$15/mo (Plus). Strong for characters and narration.
  • Hume AI — Emotion-aware voices and conversational speech. Free with credits; paid from ~$3/mo.
  • Resemble AI — Voice cloning + watermarking/security features. Pay-as-you-go.

Music

  • Mureka — Full songs with vocals, stems, remix and studio. Free limited; Pro ~$8/mo (annual).
  • Stable Audio (Stability AI) — Instrumentals, SFX, multi-track. Free 50 credits/mo; Solo $12/mo. Commercially safer data.
  • Flow Music (formerly Riffusion) — Full songs, cheaper paid tier. ~$6/mo.
  • Treblo (formerly Sonauto) — Unlimited-style generation with free options.
  • Beatoven — Scene-matched background / soundtrack music. Freemium.

Quick Recommendations

  • Longer or more consistent cinematic clips: Seedance or PixVerse
  • Cheapest strong video value: Wan or PixVerse
  • Expressive or directed voice: Fish Audio or Hume
  • Full original songs with vocals: Mureka
  • Safe instrumental / SFX for video: Stable Audio
  • Multi-model testing in one place: OpenArt

Last updated: early November 2026. Prices and features change frequently.
