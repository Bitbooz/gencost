Voice AI Pricing Comparison Updated for 2026

Voice cloning pricing is messy. Every provider bundles "characters," "credits," or "minutes" differently, and the real cost per minute of generated audio can vary by 4-5x depending on which plan you're locked into. We broke down the three most popular voice cloning platforms — ElevenLabs, Cartesia, and PlayHT — into a single comparable unit: approximate cost per minute of generated speech.

Quick math note: Most platforms bill in "characters," and roughly 1,000 characters ≈ 1 minute of spoken audio at a natural pace. We use that ratio throughout this article to normalize pricing across providers.
