Cartesia is cheapest for high-volume cloning, ElevenLabs wins for quality-per-dollar at low volume

If you're generating under 100,000 characters/month, ElevenLabs' Starter plan ($5/mo) offers the best cost-per-minute. Past that, Cartesia's per-token pricing becomes dramatically cheaper for scale.
