Runway Pricing

AI Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

In short: Runway costs from $15 a month (Standard), or $12 a month if you pay yearly. The biggest plan, Max, is $95 a month. There is also a free plan. Checked 2026-11-13 on the official pricing page.

About Runway

Runway is an AI video studio known for its own Gen-4.5 model, strong editing tools and camera control. It also offers other models such as Kling and Seedance inside the same app.

Who it's for

Filmmakers and agencies who need control over motion and camera, and are willing to pay more per clip.

Free plan

The Free plan gives a one-time 125 credits (they don't refill) to test the tools, with 5 GB of storage.

How the credits work

Gen-4.5 uses 12 credits per second of video, so a 10-second clip costs 120 credits. On Standard and Pro, credits reset every month; on Max, up to one month of unused credits rolls over. Extra credits are sold in packs of at least 1,000.

Runway pricing FAQ

How much does Runway cost?

The cheapest paid Runway plan is Standard at $15 a month ($12 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Max at $95 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-13.

Does Runway have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 125 credits. The Free plan gives a one-time 125 credits (they don't refill) to test the tools, with 5 GB of storage.

How much does one video clip cost on Runway?

On the Standard plan, one 10-second clip with Gen-4.5 costs about $2.88 (120 credits). We work this out from Runway's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $8.64 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Runway?

Standard and Pro credits reset every month (only Max rolls over 1 month). Free credits are a one-time 125, not monthly. Extra credits: minimum purchase 1,000.
