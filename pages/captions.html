About Captions

Captions is an AI video editing app for talking videos: auto captions, eye contact, dubbing, and generated B-roll, music and sound effects.

Who it's for

Creators who film themselves talking and want quick, polished edits.

Free plan

There is a free version with a more limited set of features.

How the credits work

Generative features use credits. Unused credits roll over: your balance can hold up to three times your monthly amount.
