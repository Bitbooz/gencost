About VEED

VEED is an online video editor with AI tools: subtitles, translation, AI voices, avatars and AI video generation.

Who it's for

Editing social videos in the browser, with subtitles and translation.

Free plan

You can start for free; paid plans remove the watermark.

How the credits work

Paid plans include AI credits: Creator 6,000 (VEED says about 1,500 AI videos), Pro 18,000. Prices are per user.
