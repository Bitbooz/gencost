About Fish Audio

Fish Audio is an AI text-to-speech and voice-cloning platform with a large library of community voices and an API.

Who it's for

Cheap, natural voice-overs and voice cloning.

Free plan

The Free plan gives some free generations a month, for personal, non-commercial use only.

How the credits work

One minute of speech costs about 600 to 625 credits. Monthly quotas reset each billing cycle; unused minutes don't roll over. Commercial use needs a paid plan.

Fish Audio pricing FAQ

How much does Fish Audio cost?

The cheapest paid Fish Audio plan is Plus at $15 a month ($11 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Pro at $100 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does Fish Audio have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0 and includes 8,000 credits. The Free plan gives some free generations a month, for personal, non-commercial use only.

Are there hidden costs with Fish Audio?

Free plan is for personal, non-commercial use only. Unused minutes don't roll over.
