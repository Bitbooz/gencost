Google Veo Pricing

AI Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

In short: Google Veo Google AI Pro costs $19.99 a month, or $16.67 a month if you pay yearly. There is a free option with limited use (see below). Checked 2026-11-11 on the official pricing page.

Plans

Google AI Pro: $19.99/mo · 1,000 Flow credits · 8s clip ≈ $0.40

About Google Veo

Veo is Google's video model. You use it in Google Flow, which is included in the Google AI plans together with Gemini, storage and other Google AI tools. Veo makes realistic clips with sound.

Who it's for

Realistic clips with sound, and anyone who would use Gemini and Google's other AI tools anyway.

Free plan

Students can get Google AI Pro free for one year.

How the credits work

Google AI Pro includes 1,000 Google Flow credits a month. Veo Fast uses about 20 credits per 8-second clip; the highest-quality setting uses about five times as many.

Google Veo pricing FAQ

How much does Google Veo cost?

Does Google Veo have a free plan?

How much does one video clip cost on Google Veo?

On the Google AI Pro plan, one 8-second clip with Veo Fast in Flow costs about $0.40 (20 credits). We work this out from Google Veo's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $1.20 per kept clip.
