About InVideo

InVideo AI turns a prompt or script into a finished video with scenes, voice-over and music, and gives access to generative models such as Seedance and Nano Banana Pro at their API prices.

Who it's for

Marketers and YouTubers who want complete videos from a script, not just single clips.

How the credits work

Credits pay for video creation and generative models. Seedance 2.5 at 720p is about 56 credits per 5 seconds, and it is not included on Starter. Unused credits don't roll over, and InVideo says credit costs can change without notice.

InVideo pricing FAQ

How much does InVideo cost?

The cheapest paid InVideo plan is Starter at $20 a month ($10 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Max at $150 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-10.

How much does one video clip cost on InVideo?

On the Plus plan, one 5-second clip with Seedance 2.5, 720p costs about $1.68 (56 credits). We work this out from InVideo's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $5.04 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with InVideo?

Unused credits don't roll over. Credit costs per model can change without notice.
