Which alternative should you pick?

D-ID: the cheapest way to make talking avatars

D-ID turns a photo or a stock avatar into a talking presenter. Lite costs about $5 a month but is for personal use only and carries a D-ID watermark. For client or company videos you need Pro, at about $16 a month, which adds a commercial license with a small AI watermark.

Synthesia: the closest match for business videos

Synthesia is HeyGen's main rival for training, onboarding and how-to videos. Starter costs $19 a month for 1,250 credits, $10 less than HeyGen Creator. Its free Basic plan lets you try it, with a watermark.

Colossyan: best for training teams

Colossyan focuses on workplace learning, with SCORM export for learning systems. The free Starter plan is generous; Professional at $59 a month removes the watermark and adds more minutes. It costs more than HeyGen Creator, but it is built for companies that make many training videos.

Captions: if you film yourself

Captions is not an avatar generator first: it edits videos of you talking, with auto captions, eye contact correction, dubbing and generated B-roll. If you are willing to be on camera, Max at $24.99 a month may replace HeyGen for social videos.

When HeyGen is still worth it

HeyGen's avatars and its video translation with lip-sync in 175+ languages are still among the best. If you translate a lot of existing videos, the extra cost can pay for itself.

Watch out

  • HeyGen: unused credits roll over for only one month on monthly plans, and are lost when you cancel.
  • D-ID Lite: personal use only, with a watermark.
  • Synthesia: unused minutes and credits don't roll over, and dubbing costs extra credits.
  • Colossyan: unused video minutes don't roll over.
