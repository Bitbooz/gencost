Cheapest AI Music Tools in 2026

AI music generators have become a cheap alternative to hiring composers or buying stock music licenses. Below is a cost breakdown of the most popular tools in 2026: Suno, Udio, AIVA, and Soundraw, plus a look at what you actually get for free.

Suno

Suno remains one of the cheapest ways to generate full songs with vocals and lyrics. The free plan gives you 50 daily credits, enough for roughly 10 song generations, but output is for personal use only.

Udio

Udio competes directly with Suno on quality and price. Its free tier is generous for casual users, but the $10/mo Standard plan is the real entry point for anyone who wants to use tracks commercially.

AIVA

AIVA targets composers and instrumental/background music rather than vocal pop songs. It's pricier than Suno or Udio, but better suited for royalty-free instrumental tracks and custom styles.

Soundraw

Soundraw focuses on royalty-free background music for videos and content creators. There's no free download tier, but the Creator plan is one of the simplest flat-rate options for unlimited downloads.

Which Is Cheapest Overall?

If budget is the main concern, Suno and Udio tie as the cheapest paid options at $10/month, and both offer usable free tiers for personal projects. AIVA and Soundraw cost more but serve a different need: clean, royalty-free instrumental tracks for commercial video and content work.

  • Best free tier for casual use: Suno (50 credits/day)
  • Cheapest commercial license: Suno or Udio at $10/mo
  • Best for instrumental/background music: Soundraw
  • Best for classical/custom composition: AIVA
