Tool Breakdowns

Suno

Free: 50 credits/day Pro: $10/mo Premier: $30/mo

Suno remains the cheapest way to get usable full songs with vocals. The free tier gives roughly 10 songs/day for non-commercial use, which is generous for hobbyists. The $10/mo Pro plan unlocks commercial rights and ~2,500 credits/month (roughly 500 song generations), making the per-song cost among the lowest in the market.

  • Best for: budget creators, YouTube background music, quick demos
  • Free tier commercial use: No

Udio

Free: ~10 songs/mo Standard: $10/mo Pro: $30/mo

Udio's free plan is more limited than Suno's but produces strong audio quality. The $10/mo Standard plan gives around 1,200 credits monthly (roughly 120–150 full songs), putting its per-song cost slightly higher than Suno but still extremely cheap compared to licensing stock music.

  • Best for: higher-fidelity output on a similar budget to Suno
  • Free tier commercial use: No

AIVA

Free: 3 downloads/mo Standard: $15/mo Pro: $49/mo

AIVA focuses on instrumental and orchestral composition rather than full vocal tracks, targeting game devs, filmmakers, and composers. The free plan is usable only for non-commercial downloads with limits. The Standard plan at $15/mo allows 15 downloads with commercial rights, which works out to roughly $1/track if you use the full quota.

  • Best for: instrumental scores, film/game composers
  • Free tier commercial use: No

Soundraw

Free: unlimited creation, no download Creator: $16.99/mo Artist: $23.99/mo

Soundraw lets you generate and preview tracks for free indefinitely, but you must subscribe to actually download. It's priced closer to a royalty-free stock music subscription than a pay-per-generation tool — best value if you need consistent monthly downloads rather than occasional one-off tracks.

  • Best for: content creators needing regular royalty-free background music
  • Free tier commercial use: No (preview only)
