Cheapest AI Music Tools in 2026

AI music generators have become a crowded market, with tools ranging from free experimental generators to professional-grade licensing platforms. This guide breaks down the real-dollar costs of the most popular AI music tools in 2026: Suno, Udio, AIVA, and Soundraw, along with their free tier limitations.

Quick Price Comparison

Tool Free Tier Cheapest Paid Plan Top Plan Commercial Use
Suno 50 credits/day (~10 songs) $10/mo (2,500 credits) $30/mo (10,000 credits) Paid plans only
Udio ~10 songs/month $10/mo (Standard) $30/mo (Pro) Paid plans only
AIVA 3 downloads/month (non-commercial) $15/mo (Standard) $49/mo (Pro) Standard plan and up

Suno

Suno remains one of the most popular text-to-song generators. Its free tier allows casual experimentation but restricts commercial use and limits daily generations.

Plan Price Credits Notes
Free $0 50/day Non-commercial, Suno watermark/attribution
Pro $10/mo 2,500/mo Commercial rights included
Premier $30/mo 10,000/mo Priority generation, higher quality exports

Udio

Udio competes closely with Suno on quality and pricing. Its free plan is useful for testing but has monthly caps that make it impractical for regular use.

Plan Price Generations Notes
Free $0 ~10 songs/mo Non-commercial use only
Standard $10/mo Higher monthly cap Commercial license included
Pro $30/mo Highest monthly cap Faster generation, extended track length

AIVA

AIVA is geared more toward composers and creators needing instrumental tracks for film, games, or background music. It's priced higher than Suno/Udio but offers more granular control.

Plan Price Downloads Notes
Free $0 3/mo Non-commercial, AIVA retains some rights
Standard $15/mo 15/mo Commercial license, MP3 downloads
Pro $49/mo Unlimited Full ownership rights, MIDI/WAV export

Soundraw

Soundraw targets content creators needing royalty-free background music. There's no true free download tier, only song previews before committing to a subscription.

Plan Price Downloads Notes
Monthly $16.99/mo Unlimited Billed monthly
Annual $23.99/mo (billed yearly, ~$287.88/yr) Unlimited Cheaper long-term, full commercial license

Which Is the Cheapest Overall?

  • Best free option: Suno's free tier offers the most generations per day (50 credits), though non-commercial.
  • Cheapest paid commercial option: Suno and Udio tie at $10/month for commercial-use plans.
  • Best for instrumental/background music: AIVA's $15/month plan balances cost and quality control.
  • Best for high-volume content creators: Soundraw's annual plan at $23.99/month offers unlimited downloads, which can be cheaper than paying per-credit on Suno or Udio at scale.

Final Thoughts

For hobbyists just experimenting, Suno's free tier is hard to beat. For creators needing commercial rights on a budget, Suno and Udio's $10/month plans are the cheapest entry points. If you need unlimited, royalty-free background music for videos or podcasts, Soundraw's annual plan offers the best value despite a higher sticker price. AIVA remains the priciest but most flexible option for composers needing fine-tuned instrumental control.
