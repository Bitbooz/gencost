Cheapest AI Music Tools in 2026

AI music generation has exploded in popularity, but pricing models vary wildly between platforms. Some charge per song, others use monthly credit systems, and a few still offer generous free tiers. Below we break down the real costs of the top AI music tools in 2026: Suno, Udio, AIVA, and Soundraw.

Suno Pricing Breakdown

Suno remains one of the most affordable options for casual users. The free tier allows roughly 10 songs per day with basic commercial rights restrictions. The Pro plan at $10/month unlocks more generations and faster processing, while the Premier plan at $30/month offers the highest volume for creators producing music at scale.

Free $0 50 credits/day Limited
Pro $10/month 2,500 credits/month Yes
Premier $30/month 10,000 credits/month Yes

AIVA Pricing Breakdown

AIVA targets composers and media creators needing more control over orchestration and licensing. It is pricier per track than Suno or Udio but offers deeper customization for instrumental and cinematic music.

Soundraw uses a flat monthly fee rather than credits, making it predictable for high-volume users like YouTubers and video editors. However, there is no free download option, only previews.

Which Tool Is Cheapest Overall?

For pure cost-per-song efficiency, Suno and Udio are the clear winners at roughly $0.08–$0.25 per track when using their $10/month plans. If you need unlimited generations without worrying about credits, Soundraw's flat $16.99/month annual plan can be cheaper for high-volume users who generate more than 100 songs monthly. AIVA is the most expensive option per track but offers more musical control for composers who need instrumental precision.

Best Free Tier for Beginners

  • Suno: Best overall free tier with daily credit resets.
  • Udio: Good monthly free allowance, ideal for casual hobbyists.
  • AIVA: Limited but useful for testing orchestral composition tools.
  • Soundraw: No free downloads, but useful for previewing styles before committing.
