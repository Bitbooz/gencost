Cheapest AI Music Tools in 2026

A real-dollar cost breakdown of Suno, Udio, AIVA, Soundraw, and what you actually get for free.

AI music generation pricing hasn't converged the way text and image tools have — some platforms charge per song, others per "credit," and a few still offer genuinely usable free tiers. Below we break down what each service actually costs per track, not just the sticker price on the plan page.

Suno

Suno remains the cheapest way to generate a large volume of usable tracks. The free tier gives 50 credits daily (roughly 10 song generations), which resets every 24 hours — enough for casual use without ever paying.

Free

$0 50 credits/day · non-commercial · Suno watermark on some outputs

Pro

$10/mo 2,500 credits/mo · ~500 songs · commercial rights included

Premier

$30/mo 10,000 credits/mo · ~2,000 songs · priority generation

Real cost per song: On Pro, $10 ÷ ~500 songs ≈ $0.02 per generation (each song uses ~5 credits for 2 variations). This makes Suno the single cheapest option for bulk output among tools with commercial licensing.

Udio

Udio's pricing structure mirrors Suno's but with slightly fewer free generations and marginally higher cost per track at the entry tier.

Free

$0 ~10 songs/mo · non-commercial · lower generation priority

Standard

$10/mo ~1,200 credits · commercial license · standard queue

Pro
