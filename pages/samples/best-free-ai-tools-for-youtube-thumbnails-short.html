SHORT RECOMMENDATIONS

Best overall

Canva. Fastest path from idea to finished thumbnail, with sizing and templates built in.

Best for AI visuals

Microsoft Designer or Leonardo.Ai for eye-catching backgrounds you can drop into Canva.

Best for text

Adobe Express. Sharp typography and effects that stay readable at small sizes.

Best free workflow

Generate the background in Designer, cut out your subject with remove.bg, then assemble in Canva at 1280×720.
