1. Article

Title: Cheapest AI B-Roll Generators for YouTube in 2026

Meta description: Compare the cheapest AI B-roll generators for YouTube in 2026, with real monthly costs, cost-per-usable-clip math, and three budget stacks from $0 to about $50.

Article content

The sticker price of an AI video tool is not what B-roll costs you. Most tools sell credits, and a share of generations come out unusable (warped hands, odd motion, wrong framing). If a plan gives you 50 clips for $10 and half are keepers, your real cost is about $0.40 per usable clip, not $0.20. Short 5 to 10 second clips make this workable for B-roll, since you only need cutaways under a voiceover, not full scenes. Prices change often and vary by region, so treat the numbers below as ballpark figures and check each pricing page before you subscribe.

Budget stack 1, $0 to $5 per month: Use free stock libraries like Pexels and Pixabay for roughly 70% of your cutaways. Add free daily credits from tools that offer them (Kling, Hailuo, Luma, and Pika have run free tiers or trial credits at various times) for the shots stock can't cover. Edit in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve free. Expect slow queues, watermarks on some tools, and limited commercial rights, so read the license terms before monetizing.

Budget stack 2, about $10 to $20 per month: Pick one paid mid-tier plan, such as the entry plans from Kling, Hailuo, Luma, Pika, or Runway, which have typically landed around $7 to $15. Pair it with free stock and a free editor. This is the best value for most small channels, because one tool with commercial rights and no watermark covers a weekly video. Spend credits on 5-second clips and reuse them with slow zooms and color grading to stretch them.

Budget stack 3, about $30 to $50 per month: Combine one entry-tier video generator with a general AI subscription you may already pay for (ChatGPT Plus or Google AI Pro, both around $20), which can include video generation, plus an image tool for stills. Generate still images and animate them with Ken Burns motion or image-to-video. That is often cheaper and more consistent than text-to-video. Cancel any tool you didn't use in the last 30 days.
