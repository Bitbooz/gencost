For channels publishing daily or needing higher volume, the next tier up ($20-30/month) unlocks unlimited generations and higher-quality stems on platforms like Mubert Studio or AIVA's Pro plan. But for 90% of small-to-mid YouTube creators, staying in the $0-15/month range covers every video without touching copyright-flagged content. The real cost comparison isn't AI vs. AI — it's AI generators vs. the old $20-50 per-song licensing model, and that's where the savings actually show up on your monthly budget.
