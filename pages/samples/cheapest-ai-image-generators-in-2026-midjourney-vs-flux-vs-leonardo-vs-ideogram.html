Title: Cheapest AI Image Generators in 2026: Midjourney vs Flux vs Leonardo vs Ideogram

Meta description: Compare the estimated cost of Midjourney, Flux, Leonardo, and Ideogram in 2026. See which AI image generator is cheapest for hobbyists, creators, and high-volume users.

Article content

Which AI image generator is cheapest depends on how many images you make and how picky you are about quality. Below are estimated prices for the four big names. Plans, credit allowances, and per-image costs change often, so check each official pricing page before you subscribe.

**Estimated pricing snapshot (approximate, verify before buying)**

- **Midjourney:** Plans start around $10/month (Basic) and rise to roughly $30 (Standard), $60 (Pro), and $120 (Mega). Annual billing is usually discounted by about 20%. There is no meaningful free tier. Higher plans add "Relax" mode for unlimited slower generations, which is the best value for heavy users.
- **Flux (Black Forest Labs):** Flux is priced per image through the API or third-party hosts, not as a flat subscription. Typical estimates run from about $0.003 per image (Schnell) to about $0.03–$0.05 per image (Pro and Kontext-style models). Open-weight versions can run locally for free if you have a capable GPU.
- **Leonardo AI:** A limited free daily token allowance, with paid plans starting around $10–$12/month and climbing to about $30 and $60 for larger token pools and more concurrent generations.
- **Ideogram:** A limited free tier, with paid plans around $8/month (Basic), $20/month (Plus), and $60/month (Pro). Higher tiers add more priority generations and extra features.

**Who wins on price?**

- **Lowest cost for light use:** Ideogram Basic or Leonardo's free tier. Ideogram is also strong at text in images.
- **Lowest cost per image at scale:** Flux through the API, or self-hosted if you own the hardware.
- **Best value for heavy, high-quality use:** Midjourney's higher tiers with Relax mode, if you value its aesthetic.
- **Best all-rounder with a free entry point:** Leonardo, which suits people who want to test before paying.

**Hidden costs to watch**

- Rerolls and failed generations eat credits quickly.
- Upscaling, video, and editing tools often cost extra credits.
- Commercial-use rights and privacy (private generations) can depend on plan level.
- Running Flux locally has no per-image fee, but you pay for GPU hardware and electricity.

**Bottom line:** For most casual creators, Ideogram or Leonardo is the cheapest way in. For volume, Flux wins on per-image cost. Midjourney costs more at the entry level, but it can be the best value if you generate a lot and care most about style. Always do the math with your own monthly image count.
