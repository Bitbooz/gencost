1. Article

Title: Cheapest AI Video Upscaling Tools in 2026: Topaz vs Magnific vs Runway vs CapCut

Meta description: Compare AI video upscaling and enhancement tools in 2026. See real Runway pricing, how Topaz, Magnific and CapCut differ, and which fits your budget and workflow.

Article content

AI video upscaling can turn soft 720p or 1080p footage into sharper, cleaner clips. But the four tools here charge in very different ways, so the cheapest one depends on how much footage you process.

**How the pricing models differ**
- **Runway** uses monthly plans with credits. Credits are spent per generation or processing task, so cost scales with usage.
- **Topaz** is desktop software built around local processing. It suits people who upscale often and want control over the results. Check Topaz's site for current licensing and pricing.
- **Magnific** is known for creative, detail-adding AI enhancement. Check Magnific's site for current plans and limits.
- **CapCut** is an all-in-one editor with AI enhancement features, and it offers both free and paid tiers. Check the app for what is included in each.

**Runway pricing (from GenCost data)**
| Plan | Price | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0/mo | 125 credits |
| Standard | $12/mo | 625 credits |
| Pro | $28/mo | 2,250 credits |
| Max | $76/mo | 9,500 credits |

Per credit, the plans work out to roughly $0.019 (Standard), $0.012 (Pro) and $0.008 (Max). Heavier plans cost less per credit, but only if you use the credits.

**Which one is cheapest for you?**
- **Occasional clips:** Start with free tiers and the Runway Free plan (125 credits) to test quality before paying.
- **Regular creators:** Runway Standard at $12/mo is a low-commitment option. Pro at $28/mo makes sense if you run out of credits.
- **Batch or archival work:** Desktop software like Topaz can be better value when you process a lot of footage, since you aren't metered per clip.
- **Quick social edits:** CapCut is convenient if you already edit there.

**Bottom line:** Test the same 10-second clip in each tool and compare the result against what you would pay each month. Prices change often, so confirm current rates on each vendor's site before buying.
