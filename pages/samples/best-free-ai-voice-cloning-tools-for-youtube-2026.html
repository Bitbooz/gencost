PlayHT and Murf both offer similar free character caps, enough to test voice quality before committing to a paid plan.

For creators building a real channel, the smart budget stack is: ElevenLabs free tier for your main narration voice ($0), CapCut's built-in voice tools for quick edits ($0), and Audacity for cleanup ($0). That's a genuinely $0/month voiceover pipeline for anyone making 1-2 videos weekly under 10 minutes long. Once you cross into daily uploads or longer videos, you'll hit the character wall fast — that's when upgrading to ElevenLabs Creator ($11/month) or PlayHT Pro ($39/month) starts making financial sense, since the cost-per-minute of cloned voice drops significantly at scale.

The real math: if you're uploading 4 long-form videos a month at 10+ minutes each, free tiers will run out around video 2 or 3. At that point, $11-$22/month on a paid tier is cheaper than hiring a voiceover artist ($50-$150 per video) or spending hours recording and re-recording yourself. For anyone just starting out or testing a channel concept, though, the free stack above is enough to validate the idea before spending anything.
