1. Article

Title: Best Free AI Script Writers for YouTube 2026

Meta description: Compare the best free AI script writers for YouTube in 2026, plus $0, $20, and $40 monthly budget stacks so you know exactly what a script workflow costs.

Article content

You do not need to pay for a script tool to publish a solid YouTube video. The free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot can all draft hooks, outlines, and full scripts. The tradeoff is usage limits: free plans cap messages, slow down at peak times, and sometimes lock you out of the best model. For a creator posting one or two videos a week, that is often enough. Limits and features change often, so check each tool's pricing page before you commit.

Here is the $0 stack. Use one free chatbot for the outline and first draft, a second free chatbot to critique and tighten it, and YouTube Studio's free analytics to see which of your past videos kept viewers watching. Add DaVinci Resolve or CapCut (free tiers) for editing and Canva's free plan for thumbnails. Total: $0 a month, or $0 a year.

If you hit limits, the $20 stack is the usual first upgrade. One paid chatbot plan (most are around $20 a month) removes most caps and gives you access to stronger models. That is about $240 a year, and you can cancel in months when you are not publishing. A $40 stack adds roughly $5 for a starter AI voiceover plan (for example ElevenLabs) and about $15 for Canva Pro. That is about $480 a year, and it only makes sense if you are faceless, batching videos, or selling sponsorships.

The real cost is not the subscription but your editing time. AI scripts tend to sound generic, so budget 30 to 60 minutes per video to add your own stories, examples, and opinions. Spend your money only after the free stack becomes the bottleneck, and treat any paid plan as a one-month test.
