Article content

AI music tools can look cheap until you hit credit limits, download restrictions or licensing terms. This guide compares five popular options (Suno, Udio, Soundraw, AIVA and Boomy) so you can find the best value for your budget.

**Suno pricing (GenCost data)**
- **Free:** $0/mo, 50 credits per day
- **Pro:** $8/mo, 2,500 credits
- **Premier:** $24/mo, 10,000 credits

Based on those numbers, Pro works out to about $0.0032 per credit and Premier to about $0.0024 per credit. Premier is cheaper per credit, but only if you will actually use the extra volume. The free plan is a good way to test quality before paying anything.

**Udio, Soundraw, AIVA and Boomy**
Current GenCost pricing for these four tools was not included in this package, so no figures are listed here. Before publishing, add each tool's plans from GenCost and compare them on the same basis.

**What to compare beyond the monthly price**
1. **Cost per usable song:** Credits or generations you throw away still cost money.
2. **Commercial rights:** Check whether the plan allows monetized YouTube, streaming or client work.
3. **Download quality and formats:** Some plans limit stems or high-quality files.
4. **Generation limits:** Daily and monthly caps matter more than headline prices.
5. **Use case:** Vocal songs, background music for videos and instrumental composition are different jobs, and each tool suits some better than others.

**How to choose**
- **Testing for free:** Start with Suno's free tier (50 credits per day) and see whether the output fits your style.
- **Regular creator on a budget:** Suno Pro at $8/mo is the entry paid tier in this comparison.
- **Heavy use:** Suno Premier at $24/mo gives 10,000 credits at the lowest cost per credit of the Suno plans.
- **Other tools:** Compare Udio, Soundraw, AIVA and Boomy using their current GenCost prices and licensing terms.

Always confirm the latest prices and license terms on each provider's site before subscribing.
