1. Article

Title: Runway vs Pika vs Luma: Cheapest AI Video Generator for YouTube Shorts (2026)

Meta description: Compare Runway, Pika, and Luma pricing for YouTube Shorts in 2026. See plan costs, credit allowances, and cost per credit to find the cheapest AI video generator for your channel.

Article content

If you publish YouTube Shorts daily, the cheapest AI video generator is not always the one with the lowest monthly price. Each platform uses its own credits, so you need to look at both the plan price and what you get for it.

**Entry-level pricing (GenCost data)**

| Tool | Cheapest paid plan | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Runway | Standard: $12/mo | 625 |
| Pika | Starter: $10/mo | 600 |
| Luma | Plus: $30/mo | 10,000 |

Runway has a Free plan ($0, 125 credits). Pika's Free plan is $0 but includes 0 credits. The Luma plans listed here start at Plus.

**Full plan breakdown**

- **Runway:** Free $0 (125 credits) · Standard $12 (625) · Pro $28 (2,250) · Max $76 (9,500)
- **Pika:** Free $0 (0) · Starter $10 (600) · Creator $35 (2,600) · Fancy $95 (8,550)
- **Luma:** Plus $30 (10,000) · Pro $90 (40,000) · Ultra $300 (150,000)

**Price per 100 credits (calculated from the plan prices above)**

- Runway: Standard $1.92 · Pro $1.24 · Max $0.80
- Pika: Starter $1.67 · Creator $1.35 · Fancy $1.11
- Luma: Plus $0.30 · Pro $0.23 · Ultra $0.20

**What this means for Shorts creators**

- **Lowest monthly commitment:** Pika Starter at $10/mo, or Runway Standard at $12/mo. Runway's Free plan lets you test at no cost.
- **Lowest cost per credit:** Luma, by a wide margin, at every tier.
- **Big caveat:** A credit is not the same unit across platforms. Each tool charges a different number of credits per clip depending on model, length, and settings. The real cost per Short depends on how many credits one usable clip burns, including retries.

The best way to decide is to generate the same prompt on all three, note the credits spent, and divide your plan price by the number of usable clips. That is the test the video walks through.

**Quick picks**

- Just starting, low budget: Runway Free to test, then Runway Standard or Pika Starter.
- Posting often, want volume: Luma Plus offers the most credits for the money on paper.
- Heavy production: compare Runway Max ($76), Pika Fancy ($95), and Luma Pro ($90) on cost per usable clip.
