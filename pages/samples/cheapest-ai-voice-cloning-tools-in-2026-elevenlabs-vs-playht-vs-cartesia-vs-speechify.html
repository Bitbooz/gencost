1. Article

Title: Cheapest AI Voice Cloning Tools in 2026

Meta description: Comparing ElevenLabs, PlayHT, Cartesia and Speechify for voice cloning in 2026, with real ElevenLabs plan prices and a simple cost-per-credit breakdown.

Article content

Voice cloning is now cheap enough for solo creators, but the pricing pages are confusing. Every tool counts usage differently, so a low monthly price does not always mean a low cost per finished minute of audio.

This guide uses real GenCost pricing for ElevenLabs. For PlayHT, Cartesia and Speechify, check each company's current pricing page before you decide. Plans and limits change often.

ElevenLabs plans (GenCost data):
- Free: $0/mo, 10,000 credits
- Starter: $6/mo, 30,000 credits
- Creator: $22/mo, 121,000 credits
- Pro: $99/mo, 600,000 credits
- Scale: $299/mo, 1,800,000 credits
- Business: $990/mo, 6,000,000 credits

Cost per 1,000 credits (calculated from the numbers above):
- Starter: about $0.20
- Creator: about $0.18
- Pro: about $0.165
- Scale: about $0.166
- Business: about $0.165

What this tells us: the biggest drop in unit cost comes when you move from Starter to Creator, and again at Pro. After Pro the price per credit barely changes. Scale and Business mainly buy you more volume, not a cheaper rate. If you make a few videos a month, Starter or Creator is likely the sweet spot. Move to Pro only if you regularly use over 121,000 credits.

How to compare the other three tools fairly:
1. Find the cheapest plan that includes voice cloning, since many tools lock it behind a higher tier.
2. Check whether the clone is an instant clone or a professional-grade clone.
3. Convert each plan into cost per minute of finished audio, not cost per month.
4. Check commercial usage rights. This matters if you monetize on YouTube.
5. Check what happens when you run out: overage fees, a hard stop, or a forced upgrade.

Quick take: ElevenLabs has the clearest tiered pricing and a free tier for testing. Cartesia is usually pitched on speed and low latency, PlayHT on its voice library and API, and Speechify on its reading and productivity roots. Test each with the same 60-second script and judge the results by ear before you pay.
