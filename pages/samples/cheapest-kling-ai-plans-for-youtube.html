Title: Cheapest Kling AI Plans for YouTube Creators

Meta description: Compare Kling AI Standard ($10/mo, 660 credits), Pro ($37/mo, 3000 credits) and Premier ($92/mo, 8000 credits) to find the cheapest plan for YouTube videos.

Article content

If you want to use Kling AI for YouTube B-roll, intros, Shorts or full AI-generated scenes, the best plan depends on how many clips you generate each month. Here is how the three plans compare, using GenCost pricing.

Kling AI plans
- Standard: $10/mo, 660 credits
- Pro: $37/mo, 3000 credits
- Premier: $92/mo, 8000 credits

Cost per credit (calculated from the prices above)
- Standard: about $0.0152 per credit (roughly $1.52 per 100 credits)
- Pro: about $0.0123 per credit (roughly $1.23 per 100 credits)
- Premier: about $0.0115 per credit (roughly $1.15 per 100 credits)

Which plan is cheapest for YouTube?
- Lowest monthly cost: Standard at $10/mo. It suits creators who add a few AI clips to videos, such as an intro, a transition or a couple of Shorts.
- Best balance: Pro at $37/mo. You get 3000 credits, more than four times the Standard allowance, at a lower cost per credit. It suits weekly uploaders who use AI B-roll regularly.
- Lowest cost per credit: Premier at $92/mo with 8000 credits. It only makes sense if you generate a lot, such as daily Shorts, client work or many retries per clip.
