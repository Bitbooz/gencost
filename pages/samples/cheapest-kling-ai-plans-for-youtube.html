Title: Cheapest Kling AI Plans for YouTube Creators

Meta description: Compare Kling AI's Standard ($10/mo), Pro ($37/mo) and Premier ($92/mo) plans and find the cheapest option for your YouTube workflow, with cost per credit included.

Article content

If you want to add AI-generated video to your YouTube channel without overspending, Kling AI offers three paid tiers. Here is what each costs, based on GenCost data.

Kling AI plans:
- Standard: $10/mo for 660 credits
- Pro: $37/mo for 3,000 credits
- Premier: $92/mo for 8,000 credits

Cost per credit (calculated from the prices above):
- Standard: about $0.0152 per credit ($15.15 per 1,000 credits)
- Pro: about $0.0123 per credit ($12.33 per 1,000 credits)
- Premier: about $0.0115 per credit ($11.50 per 1,000 credits)

Which plan is cheapest for YouTube?
The lowest monthly price is Standard at $10. It suits creators who want B-roll, intro clips, or Shorts experiments and don't need much volume. The lowest price per credit is Premier, but you only save money if you actually use most of the 8,000 credits. Pro is the middle ground: it costs about 19% less per credit than Standard and gives you more than four times the credits.

How to choose:
- Posting occasional Shorts or a few B-roll clips per video: start with Standard.
- Publishing weekly and regenerating clips until they look right: Pro gives you room for retries.
- Running multiple channels or producing AI video in bulk: Premier has the best per-credit rate.
