1. Article

Title: Cheapest Kling AI Plans for YouTube Creators

Meta description: Compare Kling AI's Standard ($10/mo), Pro ($37/mo), and Premier ($92/mo) plans to find the cheapest option for making YouTube videos, with cost-per-credit math.

Article content

If you want to use Kling AI for YouTube B-roll, intros, Shorts, or full AI video projects, the cheapest plan depends on how many clips you generate each month. Here are the three plans, using GenCost pricing:

- Standard: $10/mo for 660 credits
- Pro: $37/mo for 3000 credits
- Premier: $92/mo for 8000 credits

Cost per credit (calculated from the prices above):
- Standard: about $0.0152 per credit
- Pro: about $0.0123 per credit
- Premier: about $0.0115 per credit

Which plan is cheapest for you?

Standard is the cheapest way in. At $10/mo it suits creators testing Kling, making a few Shorts, or adding occasional B-roll. It has the highest cost per credit, though, so heavy use gets expensive.

Pro is the value pick for most regular uploaders. At $37/mo you get 3000 credits and a lower cost per credit, which helps if you retry prompts often, since most AI video workflows involve several attempts per usable clip.

Premier is the lowest cost per credit at $92/mo for 8000 credits. It only makes sense if you produce video at high volume, such as daily Shorts, client work, or a faceless channel with heavy AI footage.

Quick rule of thumb: start with Standard if you are unsure, move to Pro once you regularly run out of credits, and choose Premier only if you consistently use most of its 8000 credits. Credit cost per generation varies by model, length, and quality settings, so check Kling's current rates before committing.
