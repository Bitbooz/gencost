# Best Cheap AI Video Tools for YouTube in 2026
Most YouTube creators don’t need the most expensive AI tools. They need something that can produce usable Shorts, B-roll, or full videos consistently without burning money on unused credits.
This guide focuses on real-dollar cost for channels that want to stay under $40/month.
## Why Cost Matters More Than Hype
Credit-based tools look cheap until you check how fast credits disappear on longer or higher-resolution clips. The tools below were chosen because they have either a usable free tier or a paid plan under $15–20 that can support weekly uploads.
## Top Cheap Tools
### CapCut
**$0 – $12/mo**  
Best free editing tool. Unlimited exports, auto-captions, and basic AI features. Most creators never need the Pro plan.
### ElevenLabs
**$0 – $5/mo (Starter)**  
Free tier covers roughly one short video. Starter plan ($5) doubles the characters and unlocks voice cloning.
### Kling AI
**$0 – $10/mo**  
Daily free credits + strong value on the Standard plan. One of the lower costs per second of generated video.
### Pika Labs
**$0 – $10/mo**  
Good for short motion clips used as B-roll. Free tier is limited but usable for testing.
### Luma Dream Machine
**$0 – $10/mo**  
Solid monthly free allowance and a competitively priced Lite plan.
## Budget Stacks
- **$0:** CapCut free + ElevenLabs free + occasional free Kling/Pika clips  
- **$15:** CapCut free + ElevenLabs Starter + Kling Standard  
- **$40:** CapCut Pro + better voice tier + more generation credits  
## Final Verdict
Start with CapCut free and ElevenLabs free. Add a $10 generator (Kling or Pika) only when you are uploading regularly. Avoid jumping to higher-end tools until the channel is making money.
Prices change often — always confirm current rates before paying.
