About Wan

Wan is Alibaba's AI video and image platform (wan.video), made by the Tongyi lab behind the Qwen models. It runs Alibaba's own Wan video models, including Wan 3.0.

Who it's for

The lowest entry price among the big video models, especially if you pay for a year.

Free plan

The Free plan gives credits through a daily check-in, one video at a time and a watermark.

How the credits work

Each plan gives credits every month (Pro 300, Premium 1,200). Paying yearly halves the price. Pro adds 1080p and videos of 10 to 30 seconds.
