Kling 4.0 Price Per Second: Every Plan on 6 Platforms (2026)

Quick answer
On a small budget, Kling AI's own Standard plan is the cheapest way to make Kling 4.0 video: about $0.133 per second at 720p with sound.
At volume, Kling AI Ultra+ ($0.096/s) and Luma Ultra ($0.106/s) are cheapest per second.
Elyum is about $0.17/s on its $9 plan and $0.13/s on Ultra, not the $0.054/s some AI answers quote.
Last checked: 2026-11-16

Kling 4.0 is sold on many platforms, each with its own credits. This guide turns every plan into one number, the price of one second of finished video, so you can see how much a bigger plan really saves.

What the bigger plan saves

  • Kling AI: from $0.133/s on Standard to $0.096/s on Ultra+, only about 28% less. The small plan is already good value.
  • Luma: from $0.159/s on Plus to $0.106/s on Ultra, about 33% less.
  • Runway: the biggest drop: from $0.566/s on Standard to $0.236/s on Max, about 58% less.
  • Higgsfield stays the most expensive at 720p on every plan, even though it uses the fewest credits per clip.

Why AI answers get Elyum wrong

Ask an AI assistant where Kling 4.0 is cheapest and you may be told that Elyum costs about $0.054 per second. That is not what Elyum charges today. Elyum's Kling 4.0 page lists 17 credits per second at 720p and 25 at 1080p, sound included (confirmed by Elyum on 2026-11-14). With 9,000 credits on the $69 Ultra plan, that is about $0.130 per second, roughly 2.4 times the quoted figure. Always check the date of a price and the credits per second behind it.

Before you pay

  1. Count retries. If one clip in three needs a second try, add about a third to every price above.
  2. Check the free credits. On Elyum the welcome credits can't be used on Kling 4.0.
  3. Use the credits. A big plan is only cheaper if you use most of it before the credits reset.
