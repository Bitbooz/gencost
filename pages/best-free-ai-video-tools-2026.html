Why Free Tiers Keep Shrinking

AI video generation is expensive to run — far more GPU-intensive than text or even image generation. As a result, every provider on this list has quietly reduced free credits, added watermarks, or introduced daily caps over the past year. If you're trying to use these tools for free in 2026, you need to know exactly what's left before you plan a project around them.

Below we break down the real free-tier limits (not the marketing copy) for the five most popular AI video platforms, plus a few smaller free-friendly alternatives worth knowing about.

Quick take: Pika and Luma Dream Machine currently offer the most usable free generations per month. Runway's free tier is essentially a trial now. Kling gives generous daily credits but with the longest queue times on the free plan.
