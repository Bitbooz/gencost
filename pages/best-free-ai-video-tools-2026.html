Best Free AI Video Tools in 2026: Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma & More Compared

Every major AI video generator still offers some kind of free tier in 2026 — but they're shrinking fast, and the fine print varies wildly. Here's what you actually get for $0, and when you'll be forced to pay.

Quick answer
Kling, PixVerse and Vidu still give free credits you can use regularly, Kling and PixVerse with a fresh allowance every day. Pika's free plan has no watermark but no commercial license. Runway's free plan is a one-time trial, and Luma no longer has a free plan at all.

Why Free Tiers Keep Shrinking

AI video generation is expensive to run, far more GPU-intensive than text or even image generation. Over the past year providers have cut free credits, added watermarks or dropped free plans altogether. If you want to make video for $0, check exactly what is left before you plan a project around it.

Tool-by-Tool Breakdown

Kling AI Best daily volume

Kling refills a small credit balance every day, so it suits making a clip or two daily rather than one big project. Free videos carry a watermark and can't be used commercially, and free jobs wait longer in the queue. The cheapest paid plan, Standard, removes the watermark and adds commercial use.

PixVerse Daily credits

PixVerse also gives free credits every day. They renew at midnight UTC and are lost at the end of the day if you don't use them, so use them daily or not at all.

Vidu

Vidu has a free plan for trying the models. For watermark-free downloads and commercial use you need a paid plan.

Pika No watermark

Pika's free plan has no watermark, which is rare, but it has no commercial license: fine for practice, not for client work or monetized videos. A commercial license starts with the Starter plan.

Runway

Runway's free plan is a trial: 125 credits once, which don't refill. With Gen-4.5 at 12 credits per second, that is about 10 seconds of video.

Luma and Google Veo

Luma has dropped its free plan; the cheapest way in is Plus. Google Veo has no free plan either, but students can get Google AI Pro, which includes Veo, free for one year.

When you will have to pay

  • Commercial use: none of the free plans above allow it, so monetized or client videos need a paid plan.
  • No watermark: only Pika's free plan is watermark-free.
  • Volume: daily credits cover a clip or two; anything more needs a plan. The table at the top shows today's cheapest paid plan for each tool.
