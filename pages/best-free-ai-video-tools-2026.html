Best Free AI Video Tools 2026

AI video generation has become one of the most competitive spaces in creative tech, and most major platforms still offer a free tier to attract new users. Below is an up-to-date breakdown of what you actually get for free from Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, and other popular tools in 2026, along with real-dollar context for when you need to upgrade.

Kling AI Free Tier

Kling resets a small daily credit allowance, which is usually enough for one or two short clips per day. The free tier uses the standard model rather than the higher-quality "Pro" model, and outputs include a visible watermark. Credits do not roll over, so heavy users quickly hit the ceiling and move to a paid plan starting around $3.99/month for basic access.

Runway Free Tier

Runway gives new users a one-time allotment of 125 credits, which covers a handful of Gen-3 or Gen-4 video generations depending on settings (image-to-video uses more credits than text-to-video). Unlike many competitors, Runway does not add a watermark on free outputs, but once credits run out there's no daily refresh — you must subscribe. The Standard plan at $12/month adds significantly more monthly credits and higher resolution exports.

Pika Free Tier

Pika offers a generous-looking one-time credit bundle (150-300 depending on promotions) plus a small daily credit trickle for returning users. Free generations include a Pika watermark and are capped at lower resolutions. The $8-$10/month Standard plan removes the watermark and unlocks longer clips and faster generation queues.

Luma Dream Machine Free Tier

Luma's free plan allows up to 30 video generations per month, which resets monthly rather than daily. This makes it one of the more generous free tiers for casual users who don't need daily output. Free videos are capped at 5 seconds and carry a watermark. The Lite plan at $9.99/month removes the watermark and increases monthly generation limits.

Hailuo (MiniMax) Free Tier

Hailuo provides a small daily credit refresh, enough for a few short clips per day without ever paying. It's a solid option for testing prompts before committing to a paid plan. The paid tier starts at $9.90/month and adds higher resolution and priority rendering.

Open-Source Option: Hunyuan Video

For technically inclined users, Tencent's Hunyuan Video model is open-source and free to run indefinitely, with no watermark and no generation caps. The catch is that you need your own GPU (or rented cloud GPU, typically $0.50-$2/hour) since there's no official hosted free tier. This makes it effectively free only if you already have the hardware.

Which Free Tier Is Best?

For casual monthly use, Luma's 30 free generations per month is hard to beat. For daily tinkering, Kling and Hailuo both offer small daily refreshes that add up over time. Runway's one-time 125 credits are best for a single project burst rather than ongoing use, and Pika sits in between with both a one-time bundle and modest daily credits. If you're comfortable with GPU rental or local hardware, Hunyuan Video offers unlimited free generation with no watermark at all.
