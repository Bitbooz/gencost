Best Free AI Video Tools 2026

AI video generation has become one of the most competitive categories in generative AI, with nearly every major player offering some form of free access to attract users. But "free" rarely means unlimited. In this guide, we break down the actual free tier limits, watermark policies, and real-dollar costs if you need to go beyond the free plan for Kling, Runway, Pika, Luma, and other popular tools in 2026.

Tool-by-Tool Breakdown

Kling AI

Kling's free tier refreshes daily with 66 credits, enough for roughly 6-10 short clips depending on resolution and motion settings. Videos are capped at 5-10 seconds and carry a visible watermark. The daily refresh makes Kling one of the more generous free options for casual users who don't need to generate in bulk.

Runway

Runway gives new users a one-time allotment of 125 credits, which is roughly enough for 5-12 Gen-3 video generations depending on settings. Unlike Kling, this is not a recurring daily or monthly grant — once it's used, you'll need to pay $12/mo (annual) or $15/mo (monthly) for the Standard plan to continue.

Pika Labs

Pika offers 80 credits per day on its free plan, generating 5-second clips with a watermark. It's one of the better options for daily experimentation since credits reset every 24 hours rather than being a one-time grant.

Luma Dream Machine

Luma's free tier is capped at 30 generations per month (not daily), each limited to 5 seconds. This monthly cap makes it less suited to heavy testing but fine for occasional use. Paid plans start at $9.99/mo for the Lite tier, unlocking more generations and longer durations.

Hailuo (MiniMax)

Hailuo provides a generous daily credit pool (~100 credits) that resets every 24 hours, enough for several 6-second clips. It has quickly become a favorite among free-tier users due to its high daily refresh rate.

Vidu AI

Vidu's free plan is more limited, offering 20 credits per day for 4-second clips. It's best suited for quick tests rather than regular content production.

Which Free Tier Is Best?

  • Best for daily use: Pika Labs and Hailuo (daily credit refresh)
  • Best for one-time testing: Runway (highest quality per credit, but limited total)
  • Best for longer-term casual use: Kling AI (good daily balance)
  • Most restrictive: Vidu AI and Luma Dream Machine (monthly caps)

Things to Watch For

Free tiers almost always include a visible watermark, limit resolution (usually 720p or lower), and restrict video length to under 10 seconds. If you need commercial use rights, higher resolution, or watermark-free exports, you'll need to upgrade to a paid plan. Pricing and credit systems also change frequently as these companies compete for market share, so always check the official pricing page before relying on these numbers for budgeting.
