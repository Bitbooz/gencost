Tool-by-Tool Notes

Kling AI

Kling offers one of the more generous recurring free tiers, refreshing daily credits rather than a one-time allowance. This makes it a strong pick if you need a few short clips regularly rather than a large batch at once. Resolution and motion quality are capped below paid tiers, and outputs carry a visible watermark.

Runway

Runway's free tier is the most limited of the major players: a one-time credit grant (no recurring refresh) that is quickly used up on Gen-4 video generation. It's best for testing the tool once rather than ongoing free use. After the initial credits run out, you'll need a paid plan (starting around $12-$15/month) to continue.

Pika Labs

Pika refreshes free credits monthly, giving casual users a reasonable number of short clips without paying. Quality and resolution are capped at 720p, and a watermark is applied to all free-tier exports.

Luma Dream Machine

Luma caps free users at a fixed number of generations per month rather than a credit system, which makes usage easier to predict. Clips are limited to 5 seconds and 720p, with a watermark included.

Hailuo (MiniMax) and Vidu

These two newer entrants both use daily credit refreshes, similar to Kling, making them good options if you want small amounts of free generation spread across many days rather than a single lump sum.

Which Free Tool Should You Use?

  • Need daily small amounts of video: Kling AI or Hailuo
  • Want higher monthly volume: Pika Labs
  • Prefer predictable generation counts: Luma Dream Machine
  • Only testing once before buying: Runway

All free tiers in 2026 include watermarks and resolution caps (typically 720p or lower). If you need watermark-free exports or higher resolution, expect to move to a paid plan starting between $8 and $35/month depending on the tool.
