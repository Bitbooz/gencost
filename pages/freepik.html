Freepik Pricing

AI Image Pricing – Real Dollar Cost

In short: Freepik costs from $20 a month (Premium), or $14.50 a month if you pay yearly. The biggest plan, Pro, is $110 a month. There is no free plan. Checked 2026-11-11 on the official pricing page.

Plans

Premium: $20/mo · 20,000 credits
Plus: $45/mo · 50,000 credits
Pro: $110/mo · 125,000 credits

About Freepik

Freepik, now renamed Magnific, combines a large stock library with AI image, video and audio generators that use many models.

Who it's for

One subscription for stock assets plus many AI models.

How the credits work

Credits are given per year and last a full year, with no monthly reset. Some models are unlimited on paid plans.

Freepik pricing FAQ

How much does Freepik cost?

The cheapest paid Freepik plan is Premium at $20 a month ($14.50 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Pro at $110 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Are there hidden costs with Freepik?

Credits are yearly: heavy use can run out early. Music rights only on Plus and Pro.
