About Kling AI

Kling AI is Kuaishou's video generator. Its Kling 3.0 and newer models are known for realistic motion and good quality for the price, with 1080p and 4K output on paid plans.

Who it's for

Anyone who wants high-quality AI video at a low monthly price, from short social clips to cinematic shots.

Free plan

Kling has a free tier with a small number of daily credits. Paid plans add watermark removal, commercial use and faster generation.

How the credits work

The credits a clip uses depend on the model, the length, the resolution and whether audio is on. As a guide, a 5-second Kling 3.0 clip at 720p without audio is about 30 credits. The first month of each plan is 30% off.

Kling AI pricing FAQ

How much does Kling AI cost?

The cheapest paid Kling AI plan is Standard at $8.80 a month ($6.60 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Ultra+ at $544.99 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-10.

Does Kling AI have a free plan?

Kling has a free tier with a small number of daily credits. Paid plans add watermark removal, commercial use and faster generation.

How much does one video clip cost on Kling AI?

On the Standard plan, one 10-second clip with Kling 3.0, 720p, no audio costs about $0.80 (60 credits). We work this out from Kling AI's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $2.40 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Kling AI?

The first month is 30% off; the price shown here is what you pay after that. Watermark removal and commercial use only on paid plans.
