About OpusClip

OpusClip turns long videos into short clips for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, with AI captions, reframing and filler-word removal.

Who it's for

Podcasters and YouTubers who cut long videos into shorts.

Free plan

The Free plan adds a watermark and keeps your uploads for only 3 days.

How the credits work

Credits are used per minute of video processed. Paid plans export without a watermark and keep media longer; on Pro, unused credits roll over for up to 2 months.

Free: $0 · limited credits (watermark)
