About Hailuo AI

Hailuo AI is MiniMax's video generator, with the new MiniMax H3 model and the older Hailuo 2.3.

Who it's for

Short, dynamic clips at a low cost per second, especially on the bigger plans.

How the credits work

MiniMax H3 at 768p uses about 7 credits per second, so 1,000 credits make about 143 seconds of video; 2K costs about 1.7 times more per second. Watermark removal needs a paid plan.

Hailuo AI pricing FAQ

How much does Hailuo AI cost?

The cheapest paid Hailuo AI plan is Standard at $14.99 a month ($8.40 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Max at $184 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-13.

Does Hailuo AI have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0.

How much does one video clip cost on Hailuo AI?

On the Standard plan, one 10-second clip with MiniMax H3, 768p costs about $1.05 (70 credits). We work this out from Hailuo AI's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more. If you usually need 3 tries for every clip you keep, plan for about $3.15 per kept clip.

Are there hidden costs with Hailuo AI?

Watermark removal needs a paid plan. H3 at 2K costs about 1.7× more per second than 768p.
