Which should you pick?

Pick Mureka for songs with vocals

Mureka writes full songs with lyrics and vocals. Paid plans include full commercial rights for all your songs, even ones you made before upgrading, and unlimited MP3 downloads. Premier adds splitting songs into up to 12 stems, WAV, MIDI and DAW exports, and Mureka Studio.

Pick Stable Audio for instrumentals, sound beds and stems

Stable Audio, from Stability AI, makes instrumental tracks and sound effects up to 6 minutes long, as a full mix or as separate tracks. It is a good fit for video background music, podcasts and game audio. Its free plan includes a commercial license, which is rare.

Watch out

  • Mureka Free: no commercial rights; you need Pro or Premier to monetize songs.
  • Mureka Pro: no WAV, stem or MIDI downloads; those need Premier.
  • Stable Audio: track count depends on length; stems and long tracks use more credits.
