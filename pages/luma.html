About Luma

Luma makes video with its own Ray models (Ray3.2) and many third-party models such as Kling, Veo and Seedance, with HDR output and editing tools.

Who it's for

Creators who want several top models and clear per-second pricing in one place.

Free plan

Luma has no free plan; the cheapest plan is Plus.

How the credits work

Ray3.2 at 720p costs 100 credits for 5 seconds and 300 for 10 seconds; 1080p costs four times as much. Every plan includes commercial use.

Luma pricing FAQ

How much does Luma cost?

The cheapest paid Luma plan is Plus at $30 a month ($25 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Ultra at $300 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-07.

Does Luma have a free plan?

Luma has no free plan; the cheapest plan is Plus.

How much does one video clip cost on Luma?

On the Plus plan, one 10-second clip with Ray3.2, 720p costs about $0.90 (300 credits). We work this out from Luma's own credits per clip and today's plan price; higher resolutions and premium models cost more.

Are there hidden costs with Luma?

No free plan. 1080p costs 4× the credits of 720p (Ray3.2).
