Suno

Suno generates complete songs with vocals, lyrics and instruments from a simple text prompt. You describe the style, mood, topic and even structure, and it produces a full track.

Best for: Full songs with vocals, demos, and people who want finished tracks quickly.

Free vs Paid: Free tier gives limited daily generations and no commercial rights. Pro and Premier unlock more generations, longer songs and commercial use.

Typical use: Content creators, musicians testing ideas, and anyone who wants a complete song without recording.

Pros: Fast full songs with vocals, easy to use, good commercial options on paid plans.

Cons: Free tier is limited. Results can vary in quality.

Pricing overview: Free plan available. Paid plans start around $10–30/mo depending on generation limits and commercial rights.
