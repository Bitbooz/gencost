Suno pricing FAQ

How much does Suno cost?

The cheapest paid Suno plan is Pro at $10 a month ($8 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Premier at $30 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-11.

Does Suno have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan gives 50 credits a day with the free models, but no song downloads and no commercial rights.

Are there hidden costs with Suno?

Free: no downloads and no commercial rights. Monthly credits don't carry over; top-ups need an active plan.

How much does Suno Studio cost?

Suno Studio, with MIDI, effects and automation, is only part of the Premier plan: $30 a month, or $24 a month paid yearly. It is not included in Free or Pro.

Can I use Suno songs commercially?

Yes, on Pro and Premier, which include commercial use rights. Songs made on the Free plan have no commercial rights. Suno's terms of service list the limits.

How many songs can I download on Suno?

Free has no monthly song downloads. Pro ($10 a month) includes 2,500 credits and 20 song downloads a month. Premier ($30 a month) includes 10,000 credits and 60 downloads.

Which Suno models do you get on each plan?

Free uses the free v6-mini model. Pro and Premier get the advanced v6 and v6-wild models, and a priority queue with up to 10 songs at once.
