Udio

Udio creates high-quality music from text descriptions. It offers strong control over genre, instruments, mood and song structure, and is often praised for sound quality.

Best for: Higher quality tracks and more detailed control over the result.

Free vs Paid: Free plan available with limits. Paid plans increase generation count and unlock commercial rights.

Typical use: Producers and creators who want better sounding results and more style control.

Pros: Strong sound quality and good style control.

Cons: Free tier has limits. Can take a bit more effort to get exactly what you want.

Pricing overview: Free plan available. Paid plans increase generation limits and add commercial rights.
