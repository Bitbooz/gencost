AIVA

AIVA focuses on AI-composed music, especially classical, cinematic and emotional styles. You can also upload MIDI and let it continue, arrange or generate new parts.

Best for: Instrumental music, soundtracks and more classical or emotional styles.

Free vs Paid: Free tier exists with limits. Higher plans unlock more instruments, longer tracks and commercial rights.

Typical use: Composers, filmmakers and people who want instrumental music rather than full songs with vocals.

Pros: Strong on instrumental and soundtrack styles. MIDI support.

Cons: Less suited for songs with vocals.

Pricing overview: Free tier available. Higher plans add more instruments and commercial rights.
