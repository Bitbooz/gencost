Runway vs Kling: Pricing Comparison 2026

A side-by-side breakdown of subscription tiers, credit costs, and real-dollar generation costs for Runway and Kling — two of the leading AI video generation platforms heading into 2026.

Quick Summary

Runway and Kling remain the two most competitive players in AI video generation for 2026, but they take very different pricing approaches. Runway leans toward a premium, feature-rich subscription model built around its Gen-4 lineup, while Kling (by Kuaishou) continues to undercut on raw generation cost, particularly for longer clips and higher volume users.
