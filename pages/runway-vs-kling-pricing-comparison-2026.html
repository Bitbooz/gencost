Runway vs Kling Pricing Comparison 2026

A side-by-side breakdown of plans, credit costs, and real per-video pricing to help you pick the right AI video generator for your budget.

Runway and Kling are two of the most capable AI video generation platforms heading into 2026, but they price themselves very differently. Runway leans on a credit-based subscription model with tiered monthly plans, while Kling offers a mix of free daily credits, subscription packages, and pay-as-you-go top-ups aimed at a more global, mobile-first audience. Below we break down list prices, effective cost per video second, and which platform wins depending on your use case.

Runway's Gen-4 video generation typically consumes around 5–12 credits per second of output depending on resolution and model settings, meaning a 10-second Gen-4 Turbo clip can cost roughly 50–100 credits, or about $1–$2 in effective spend on the Pro plan.
