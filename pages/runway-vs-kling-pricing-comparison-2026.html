Runway vs Kling Pricing Comparison 2026

Runway and Kling are two of the leading AI video generation platforms heading into 2026. Both use credit-based systems tied to monthly subscriptions, but their pricing structures, generation costs, and included features differ in ways that matter for your budget. This guide breaks down the real dollar costs so you can decide which tool fits your workflow.

Which One Costs Less for Typical Use Cases

Light users (a few short clips per month): Kling's $10 Standard plan is cheaper and provides enough credits for casual experimentation.

Mid-volume creators (social content, marketing clips): Kling Pro at $25/month edges out Runway Pro at $28/month on raw credit value, but Runway's editing suite may justify the small premium for teams that need in-app post-production.

Heavy users / studios: Runway Unlimited at $76/month can outperform Kling Premier at $62/month in total value if your output regularly exceeds 75 minutes of video per month, since Runway removes the credit ceiling entirely.

Final Verdict

Kling generally offers lower cost per credit and per minute of video across comparable tiers, making it the better choice for budget-conscious creators and high-volume short-form content. Runway remains the stronger pick for users who need advanced editing tools, 4K output, and unlimited generation without credit tracking. Your best choice depends less on raw pricing and more on whether you value Runway's production toolkit or Kling's lower per-minute cost.

Pricing for both platforms changes frequently as competition in AI video tools intensifies. Always verify current rates directly on each provider's pricing page before committing to an annual plan.
