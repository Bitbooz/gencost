About Midjourney

Midjourney is one of the best-known AI image generators, used for art, concept design and illustrations.

Who it's for

High-quality, artistic images and concept art.

How the credits work

Plans include fast GPU time; unused fast hours expire each month. Images are public unless you are on Pro or Mega (Stealth Mode), and companies earning over $1M a year must use Pro or Mega.
