About HeyGen

HeyGen makes videos with AI avatars: you type a script and a realistic presenter speaks it, in 175+ languages. It is used for marketing, sales and training videos.

Who it's for

Talking-head and presenter videos without filming, and translating existing videos with lip-sync.

Free plan

The Free plan makes up to 3 videos a month, up to 1 minute each, with a HeyGen watermark.

How the credits work

Credits are HeyGen's single currency. How many a video uses depends on the AI model, the video length and how complex it is. Monthly subscribers' unused credits roll over for one month; on yearly plans they build up until renewal.

HeyGen pricing FAQ

How much does HeyGen cost?

The cheapest paid HeyGen plan is Creator at $29 a month ($24 a month if you pay yearly). The biggest plan we track is Pro at $49 a month. Prices checked 2026-11-08.

Does HeyGen have a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan costs $0. The Free plan makes up to 3 videos a month, up to 1 minute each, with a HeyGen watermark.

Are there hidden costs with HeyGen?

Free videos are watermarked. Monthly credits roll over for one month only (yearly: until renewal). Unused credits are lost when you cancel.
