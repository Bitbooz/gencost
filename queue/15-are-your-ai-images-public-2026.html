Are Your AI Images Public? Privacy on Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram and More (2026)

Many people don't know that on some AI image tools, everything they make shows up in a public gallery that anyone can browse. That matters if you're making images for a client, a product launch or anything private. Here is which plans keep your images private.

Quick answer
Midjourney: images are public on Basic and Standard. Private images (Stealth Mode) start on Pro, $60/mo.
Ideogram: free images are public; private from Plus, $20/mo.
Leonardo and Recraft: free-plan images are public; paid plans are the way out.
Last checked: 2026-11-15

Rights come with it

  • Midjourney: companies earning more than $1 million a year must use Pro or Mega anyway.
  • Leonardo: on the free plan, Leonardo holds the rights to your images.
  • Recraft: free images may not be used commercially, and commercial rights only last while you subscribe.
  • OpenArt: no commercial use on Starter.

What this means

On Midjourney, privacy means a $60 plan

Pro costs twice as much as Standard but also gives twice the fast hours, at the same price per hour. So privacy doesn't cost more per image, but you have to buy at least $60 a month to get it. If your images don't need to be secret, Standard is the cheaper way in.

For client work, budget for the private plan

A client's new logo or product photo showing up in a public gallery before launch is a real risk. The cheapest private option on this list is Leonardo Essential or Recraft Basic at about $12 a month.
