Yearly vs Monthly: How Much You Save on 30 AI Tools (2026)

Almost every AI tool offers a lower price if you pay for a year upfront, but the discount ranges from almost nothing to more than half. We compared the monthly and yearly price of the cheapest paid plan on 47 AI video, image, voice and music tools. 30 of them give a yearly discount; 17 don't.

Quick answer
Biggest saving: Speechify Premium, 60% (about $209 a year), then InVideo Starter, 50% (about $120 a year).
Typical saving: about 20%, the same as two or three months free. That is what Suno, Udio, Pika, Runway and Midjourney give.
Almost no saving: D-ID Lite and OpenArt Starter, 1% and 7%.
Last checked: 2026-11-13

Is paying yearly worth it?

Yes, if you already know the tool

If you have used a tool for a month or two and know you will keep it, a yearly plan is the easiest saving there is. On a 20% discount you get the equivalent of more than two months free. On Speechify, InVideo, Krea and Hailuo the discount is so big that a year costs about the same as five to seven months paid monthly.

No, if prices are moving fast

AI tool prices change often. In the past week alone Higgsfield lowered its yearly prices and InVideo cut its Max plan. A tool you pay a year for can get cheaper, or a rival can get better, before the year is over. Check the price changes log before you commit.

Watch out

  • Yearly credits can work differently. Some tools give a whole year's credits upfront, others refill every month. See credits and residuals.
  • Refunds are rare. Most AI tools don't refund a yearly plan if you stop using it.
  • First-month deals are not yearly discounts. A cheap first month (CapCut, Dreamina, Kling) only lowers one month.
