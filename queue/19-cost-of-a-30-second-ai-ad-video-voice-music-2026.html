What Does a 30-Second AI Ad Cost? Video, Voice and Music Together (2026)

One AI tool rarely does it all. A short ad needs moving pictures, a voice and music, and each comes from a different tool with its own plan. We put together a budget combination and a better-quality one, with commercial rights on every part.

Quick answer
Budget: Kling Standard + Cartesia Pro + Stable Audio Free = about $14 a month, enough for about 3 ads (about $2.50 of credits per ad).
Better quality: Kling Pro + ElevenLabs Starter + Suno Pro = about $49 a month, enough for about 10 ads with Kling 4.0 (about $3.85 per ad, including the song).
Last checked: 2026-11-15

What the numbers mean

The video is the expensive part

The voice and music cost a few cents per ad. Almost all the money goes to the video clips, so that is where to compare prices: see where your model is cheapest in the cheapest clip finder.

Plan for retries

The numbers assume every clip works the first time. If you make two or three tries per clip you keep, the video part costs two or three times as much: the budget plan then covers one ad a month.
