720p vs 1080p AI Video: How Much More Does HD Cost? (2026)

The resolution button is the easiest way to blow your credits. On some tools 1080p costs half as much again as 720p; on others it costs four times as much. Sound can add another 50%. Here is how much each setting costs, tool by tool.

Quick answer
For Kling 4.0, 1080p costs about 1.5× the credits of 720p on every platform we checked.
For Seedance 2.5, 1080p costs about 1.7× to 2.5× as much.
For Luma's Ray3.2, 1080p costs 4× as much as 720p.
On Kling 3.0, turning sound on adds 50%.
Last checked: 2026-11-15

How to save

Draft in low resolution, finish in high

Try your prompt at 720p (or 480p or 540p where offered) until you like the motion, then make the final version at 1080p. On Luma, ten Ray3.2 drafts at 720p cost the same as two and a half clips at 1080p.

Ask if you really need 1080p

On a phone screen, most viewers can't see the difference between 720p and 1080p in a short social clip. For TikTok, Reels and Shorts, 720p is often enough.

Add sound later if it's cheaper

On Kling 3.0, a silent clip plus music from a separate tool can be cheaper than generating sound with the video.
