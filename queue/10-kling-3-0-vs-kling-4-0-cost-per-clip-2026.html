Kling 3.0 vs Kling 4.0: How Much More Does the New Model Cost? (2026)

Kling 4.0 has been open to everyone since October 15, 2026, and Kling 3.0 is still on offer next to it. Is the new model worth the extra credits? Here is what one 5-second clip of each costs on Kling's own plans and on Higgsfield.

Quick answer
On Kling Standard: Kling 3.0 costs about $0.40 without sound and $0.60 with sound; Kling 4.0, always with sound, about $0.67 (5 seconds, 720p).
So with sound, Kling 4.0 costs only about 11% more than Kling 3.0 at 720p, and 25% more at 1080p.
On Higgsfield the jump is bigger: about $0.99 for Kling 3.0 against $1.76 for Kling 4.0 on Starter.

What the numbers mean

If you use sound anyway, Kling 4.0 is a small step up

Kling 4.0 always makes sound together with the picture. Compared with Kling 3.0 with sound switched on, it only costs a few cents more per clip on Kling's own plans.

Without sound, Kling 3.0 is still the cheaper choice

For silent clips you put music or a voiceover on later, Kling 3.0 without sound is about 40% cheaper than Kling 4.0, and the Standard plan's 660 credits give 22 clips instead of 13.

Longer clips

Kling 4.0 makes clips of 3 to 30 seconds. One 30-second 1080p clip uses about 450 credits, two thirds of the Standard plan's credits a month.

Watch out

  • Check the button. Kling shows the credit cost on the generate button before you start; check it for long or high-resolution clips.
  • First-month discount. The first month of each Kling plan is 30% off; the prices above are the normal ones.
  • Free accounts can try Kling 4.0, but with a watermark and no commercial use.
