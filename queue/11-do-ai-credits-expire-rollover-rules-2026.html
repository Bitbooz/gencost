Do AI Credits Expire? Rollover Rules for 21 AI Tools (2026)

You pay for credits every month, but what happens to the ones you don't use? On most AI tools they simply disappear on your billing date. A few let them build up, and some even take them away when you cancel. Here is what each tool does with your unused credits.

Quick answer
On most AI tools, unused monthly credits expire at the end of each billing period.
Only a few roll over: Cartesia (up to 2× your monthly amount), Captions (up to 3×), HeyGen (one month) and Runway Max (one month). Leonardo keeps unused tokens in a bank, up to a cap.
Credits you buy separately usually last longer: 365 days on Pika and Ideogram, forever on Recraft.

What this means for your budget

Pick the plan for your normal month, not your busiest

If credits expire, every credit you don't use is money gone. A plan that covers a normal month, plus a top-up in a busy one, is often cheaper than a big plan you only fill now and then. This works best on tools where top-ups last a long time, such as Recraft, Pika or Ideogram.

Tools that forgive a quiet month

If your use goes up and down, Cartesia and Captions are the most forgiving: unused credits build up to two or three months' worth. HeyGen keeps them for one extra month.

Use your credits before you cancel

On HeyGen and ElevenLabs, unused credits are lost the moment you cancel or downgrade. Use them first, then cancel.
