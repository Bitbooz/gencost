AI Tool First-Month Deals: What You Really Pay After Month One (2026)

$1 for the first month, 30% off, half price: AI tools love first-month deals. They are a good way to try a tool, but the second bill is often a surprise. Here is the first-month price and what you pay after that.

Quick answer

How to use first-month deals well

Test hard in month one

A $1 or $0.29 month is a cheap way to find out if a tool fits you. Use it fully: make the kind of clips, voices or songs you really need, and count how many credits they use.

Set a reminder before the second bill

All these plans renew automatically at the full price. Put a reminder in your calendar a few days before the renewal date, and cancel if you're not sure.

Compare the normal prices, not the deals

A tool that is cheap for one month can be expensive for a year. Dreamina Basic costs about $165 in the first year; Kling Standard about $104. GenCost always shows the normal price, so you can compare like with like.
