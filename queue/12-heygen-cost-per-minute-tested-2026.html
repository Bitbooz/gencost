What Does a HeyGen Avatar Video Really Cost? We Tested It (2026)

HeyGen sells credits, not minutes, so it's hard to know what a video will cost before you make it. So we tested it ourselves: three short videos with one of HeyGen's ready-made avatars, and we counted exactly how many credits they used.

Quick answer
Our three videos (32, 28 and 39 seconds, 99 seconds in total) used 65 credits: about 39 credits per minute.
That is about $1.90 per minute of finished video on Creator and Pro, or about $1 for a 30-second video.
Creator's 600 credits make about 15 minutes of video a month; Pro's 1,000 about 25 minutes.
Tested: 2026-11-15

What we learned

Pro is not cheaper per minute

Pro gives more credits, but at almost exactly the same price per credit as Creator. Choose Pro for its features or the bigger monthly amount, not to save per minute.

Short videos are cheap, long ones add up

A 30-second social video costs about 20 credits, so Creator covers around 30 of them a month. A 10-minute training video would use about 390 credits, two thirds of Creator in one go.

Other avatars can cost more

We used a ready-made public avatar. Custom avatars, premium avatar engines and translation use credits faster, so check the cost HeyGen shows before you generate.

Watch out

  • Credits roll over only one month on monthly plans, so don't save them up for long.
  • Unused credits are lost when you cancel. Use them first.
  • Free videos are watermarked. The free plan gives 3 videos a month.
