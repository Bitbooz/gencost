Is the Bigger AI Plan Really Cheaper? Price per Credit on 20 Tools (2026)

"Upgrade and save" is the oldest line on any pricing page. We divided every plan's price by its credits to see if it's true. Often it is, and on some tools the savings are big. But on several tools the bigger plan costs the same per credit, and on a few it's even worse.

Quick answer
The biggest savings for upgrading: Runway (Max is 58% cheaper per credit than Standard), Hailuo (Max 55%), CapCut (Ultra 45%) and InVideo (Plus 40%).
Almost no saving: HeyGen, Synthesia, Krea, Captions, Ideogram, Dreamina and Magic Hour.
Worse per credit on the bigger plan: Udio Pro and PixVerse Ultra.
Last checked: 2026-11-15

Bigger plan, worse price per credit

  • Udio: Standard gives 2,400 credits for $10/mo ($4.17 per 1,000); Pro gives 6,000 for $30/mo ($5.00 per 1,000). Pro costs 20% more per credit.
  • PixVerse: Premium is the best value at $4 per 1,000 credits. Ultra costs about $10 per 1,000, so check what extra it includes before you pay for it.

Worked out on 2026-11-15 from each plan's monthly price and monthly credits. Per-credit prices are rounded.

How to use this

Upgrade only where it pays

On Runway, Hailuo, CapCut and InVideo, moving up one plan can cut the cost of every clip by a third or more, if you really use the credits. On HeyGen, Synthesia, Krea or Captions you pay the same per credit either way, so pick the plan that fits your month.

A "price cut" can be a smaller plan

When a plan suddenly costs less, check the credits too. In November 2026 Dreamina's Advanced plan went from $159 to $79 a month, but its credits went from 17,600 to 8,645, so the price per credit hardly changed.
