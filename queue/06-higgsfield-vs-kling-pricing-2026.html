Higgsfield vs Kling: Which Is Cheaper for Kling Videos? (2026)

Higgsfield is one of the most popular places to use Kling's video models, next to many others in one app. But is it cheaper than buying from Kling itself? We worked out the cost of one clip of Kling 3.0 and Kling 4.0 on every plan of both.

Quick answer
For Kling's own models, Kling AI is about two to three times cheaper per clip than Higgsfield on every plan.
A 5-second Kling 4.0 clip at 720p: about $0.67 on Kling Standard, about $1.76 on Higgsfield Starter.
Higgsfield makes sense if you also use its other models, such as Seedance 2.5 (from about $0.65 a clip), and its editing tools.
Last checked: 2026-11-14

What the numbers mean

For Kling models, go to the source

Kling's Standard plan costs less than half of Higgsfield Starter, and every Kling clip costs about a third as much. Even Higgsfield's biggest plan, Ultra, is more expensive per Kling 4.0 clip than Kling's cheapest one.

Paying yearly narrows the gap

Higgsfield's yearly prices are much lower than its monthly ones: Plus costs about $35 a month billed yearly instead of $59. That brings a 720p Kling 4.0 clip down to about $0.73, close to Kling Standard's monthly price per clip. Kling's yearly plans are about 25% cheaper too.

Why people still pick Higgsfield

Higgsfield puts Kling, Seedance, Veo and its own tools in one app with one credit balance. Its Plus plan covers about 80 Seedance 2.5 videos, about $0.74 each, which is cheaper than Seedance 2.5 on most platforms. If you switch between models a lot, one Higgsfield plan can cost less than two or three separate subscriptions.

Watch out

  • Starter's monthly price. Higgsfield doesn't show a monthly price for Starter on its pricing page, so check the price at checkout.
  • First-month discounts. Kling gives 30% off the first month of each plan; the prices above are the normal ones.
  • Retries count. If you need three tries for every clip you keep, multiply each price by three.
