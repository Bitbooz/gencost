Cheapest AI Video Tools You Can Use Commercially (2026)

If you make videos for a client, for ads or for a monetized channel, you need a plan that allows commercial use. Many AI video tools keep that out of their free plan, and some out of their cheapest paid plan too. These are the cheapest plans where the tool itself says commercial use is included.

Quick answer
Cheapest with commercial use: Kling AI Standard, about $8.80 a month.
Also from $10: Pika Starter and Vidu Standard.
Watch out: on D-ID and LTX Studio the cheapest paid plan is personal use only; commercial use starts one plan up.
Last checked: 2026-11-13

What the rules mean

"Paid plans include commercial use"

Kling, Vidu, Magic Hour and Luma include commercial use on every paid plan, so the cheapest paid plan is enough. Kling Standard is the cheapest of all, at under $9 a month.

No watermark is not the same as commercial use

Pika's free plan has no watermark, but it also has no commercial license. A clean video is not automatically one you are allowed to sell or use in an ad.

The cheapest paid plan is not always enough

D-ID Lite and LTX Studio Lite are paid plans for personal use only. For client work you need D-ID Pro or LTX Studio Standard, which cost more than three and two times as much.

Watch out

  • Read the terms for your use. Commercial use usually covers ads and client work, but some tools have extra rules for things like reselling or training other AI models.
  • Videos made on a free plan stay free-plan videos. Upgrading later doesn't always give commercial rights to clips you made before.
  • Models inside other tools. When you use Kling, Seedance or Veo inside another platform, that platform's rules apply.
