Best Suno Alternatives in 2026: Udio, Mureka, Stable Audio and More

Suno is the best-known AI song generator, and its Pro plan costs $10 a month for 2,500 credits and 20 song downloads, with commercial use. If you need more downloads, different rights or a lower price, these are the alternatives, compared on their cheapest paid plan.

Quick answer
Cheapest: Mureka Pro, $8 a month, with unlimited downloads and commercial rights, also for songs you made before upgrading.
Closest to Suno: Udio Standard, $10 a month for 2,400 credits.
Commercial use for free: Stable Audio's free plan includes a commercial license.

Which alternative fits you

Mureka: the cheapest way to sell your songs

Mureka Pro costs less than Suno Pro and gives unlimited downloads. Its paid plans also give commercial rights to songs you made on the free plan, which Suno and Udio don't promise.

Udio: the closest match

Udio Standard costs the same as Suno Pro and gives a similar number of credits. Many people use both and keep the songs they like best.

Stable Audio and Soundraw: background music

For music under videos and podcasts rather than full songs with vocals, Stable Audio and Soundraw are made for the job. Stable Audio even allows commercial use on its free plan, and Soundraw Creator has unlimited downloads.

Watch out

  • Free plans usually can't be monetized. Suno's free plan has no downloads or commercial rights, and on AIVA's free plan AIVA owns the copyright.
  • Credits don't carry over on Suno and Udio, so an unused month is lost.
  • Read the terms for streaming. Releasing AI songs on Spotify has its own rules on each platform.
