How Much Does Krea AI Cost?

Quick answer
Krea has a free plan with 100 compute units a day and paid plans from $9/mo (Basic, 5,000 units a month). On Basic, one 5-second Kling 2.5 video costs about $0.47, and one 5-second Seedance 2.5 video at 720p about $1.80.
Last checked: 2026-11-22

Krea puts many AI video and image models in one subscription: Kling, Seedance, Veo, Wan, LTX and Krea's own image model. You pay with "compute units", and each model uses a different number of them, so the plan price alone doesn't tell you what a video costs. Here's the real price.

Is Krea a good deal?

  • Yes, if you want many models in one place. One plan covers Kling, Seedance, Veo, Wan and image models, so you don't need several subscriptions.
  • For one model only, the maker can be cheaper. If you only use Kling, compare with Kling's own plans; for Seedance, see the cheapest way to use Seedance 2.5.
  • Yearly billing saves a lot. Basic paid yearly is $5/mo instead of $9/mo, so every video above costs about 45% less.

Watch out

  • The free plan has no commercial license. You need a paid plan to use the videos for clients or ads.
  • 100 free units a day is less than one Kling 2.5 video (about 263 units). The free plan is for images and trying things out.
  • Units don't roll over. Monthly units are lost at the end of the month, and extra units you buy expire after 90 days.
  • Retries cost the same as keepers. If you need three tries per video, multiply the price by three.

Questions people ask

Is Krea free?

Yes, Krea has a free plan with 100 compute units a day, but no commercial license. Paid plans start with Basic at $9 a month, or $5 a month paid yearly.

How many videos do you get with Krea Basic?

About 19 five-second Kling 2.5 videos, or about 5 five-second Seedance 2.5 videos at 720p, from Basic's 5,000 compute units a month.

Do Krea compute units roll over?

No. Monthly units are lost at the end of each month, and units you buy on top expire after 90 days.
