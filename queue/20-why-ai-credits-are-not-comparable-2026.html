Why 1,000 AI Credits Mean Something Different on Every Tool (2026)

Every AI tool counts in its own credits, and they are not worth the same. 1,000 credits cost as little as about $1.14 on LTX Studio's Pro plan and about $125 on Viggle Pro. That doesn't make one tool 100 times cheaper: a clip simply uses a very different number of credits on each. Here is how to compare them the right way.

Quick answer
Credits are a different currency on every tool, so never compare credit counts between tools.
The same 5-second Kling 4.0 clip at 720p uses 25 credits on Higgsfield, 50 on Kling AI, 85 on Elyum and 265 on Luma, yet the dollar cost is in the same range.
Compare dollars per clip, per minute or per image instead.

How to compare AI tools the right way

  1. Pick what you make: a 5-second clip, a minute of voice, one image or one song.
  2. Find its credits on each tool, at the same model and resolution.
  3. Divide the plan price by the plan's credits and multiply by the credits per clip.
  4. Add retries: if a clip takes two tries, double it.
