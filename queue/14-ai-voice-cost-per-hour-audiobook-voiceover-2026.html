What Does an Hour of AI Voice Cost? Audiobooks and Voiceovers (2026)

AI voice tools sell characters or credits, but what you really want to know is: what does an hour of finished narration cost? We converted every plan into hours of speech, and worked out what a 10-hour audiobook would cost.

Quick answer
The cheapest per hour: Cartesia (about $1.80 to $2.40 an hour) and Fish Audio (about $1.80 to $2.20 an hour).
ElevenLabs, the best-known, costs about $8 to $10 an hour.
A 10-hour audiobook: about $20 on Cartesia or Fish Audio, about $80 on ElevenLabs.

What the numbers mean

Price isn't the only thing

ElevenLabs costs four times as much per hour, but it has the biggest choice of voices and languages and very natural speech. For a long audiobook, listen to samples from each tool before you choose: the cheapest voice is only cheap if you're happy with it.

Retakes cost credits too

Every time you regenerate a sentence, you pay again. Plan for 10 to 30% extra on a long project.

Check the rules where you publish

All the paid plans above include commercial use, but free plans don't. Audiobook stores have their own rules about AI narration, so check them before you start.
