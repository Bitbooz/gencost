FAQ: Pictory pricing
Can’t find your answer?
Contact our team

How do credits and video minutes work?
Each plan includes a credit allowance, shown alongside the video minutes it covers. For example, Professional includes 72,000 credits, which covers 1,080 minutes of video per year.
Are credits granted upfront on annual plans?
Yes. Annual plans grant all of your credits upfront, so you can use more in busy periods without waiting for a monthly renewal. Monthly plans refresh their allowance each month.
What counts as a video project?
A video project is the collection of assets behind a video, such as your script, clips and music. You can make changes and export multiple versions and it still counts as one project. The free trial lets you create 3 video projects.
Do you offer refunds?
We don’t currently offer refunds on paid subscriptions. You can cancel anytime and you won’t be charged on renewal. We also offer a 14-day free trial so you can be sure you’ve chosen the right plan.
What forms of payment and currencies do you accept?
Prices are in USD. We accept all major debit and credit cards, as well as PayPal. Payments are processed securely by Stripe and PayPal.
Is your payment system secure?
Yes. Our payment system uses the latest security technology and is powered by Stripe and PayPal, two of the world’s most secure payment companies.
Which languages does Pictory support?
Professional and Teams plans support 29 languages, and Starter plans support seven.
What happens to my videos if I cancel?
The videos you create are yours to keep, but you must download them before you cancel. Video projects are permanently deleted from the platform when your account is cancelled.
Do I need to pay royalties for visuals or music?
No. Pictory has already licensed all the video clips, music and voiceovers for use in your videos.
Do you have a non-profit discount?
Yes. Log into your Pictory account and reach out through the chat box in the bottom-right corner of your screen.
